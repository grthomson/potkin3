# Typed CK carrier audit: residues, connected quotient, and native cut coaction

11 October 2026. Paper-level audit. No Lean/Racket carrier theorem is asserted.

## Verdict

Fixed-arity typed proof trees do admit a CK construction. The clean carrier is the bialgebra of typed operadic trees (P-trees). It retains a trivial edge e_J for every complete judgment J. Those edges are degree-zero grouplikes. Collapsing **standalone forest factors** e_J to the algebra unit gives a connected CK-type Hopf algebra H. It does not remove the typed vacant ports *inside positive trees*. Keeping the right factor uncollapsed gives a genuine H-coaction on typed open trees.

Consequently there is no need to abandon CK, but the following three objects must not be conflated:

1. the typed bialgebra B with its edge residues;
2. its connected quotient H, which still has positive typed open-tree generators;
3. ordinary untyped CK, obtained by a further core/decoration-forgetting map.

The old October 5 note assumes a typed connected carrier and cut/flag laws. This audit supplies a standard route to that carrier at the paper level; it does not retrofit a native Lean declaration. The October 10 native audit correctly says that instantiated tensor coassociativity was still absent.

## 1. Registry and trees

Fix judgment sorts J and rule instances r:(J_1,...,J_n)->J, with ordered premise positions. Complete judgments include the information needed for exact local checking, not merely the displayed conclusion formula. Every port is present even when unfilled. Let e_J be the tree with one edge, no rule vertex, root/leaf color J. Positive trees have rule vertices and possibly unfilled leaf edges. Forests are ordered lists; multiplication is concatenation.

This applies directly when the registry's admissibility is hereditary and reconstruction is local: an edge connects equal complete judgments, and the stored rule instance supplies every extra occurrence/incidence datum. Any additional nonlocal condition must be separately shown hereditary or incorporated in the state. A finite recognizer needs a finite registry or a separately justified finite abstraction; the coalgebra itself does not need finiteness of the sort set.

## 2. Typed bialgebra B

For each positive tree T, sum over root-containing, arity-preserving subtrees R. Every leaf edge of R determines its entire ideal tree above that edge in T. Their left-to-right list is P_c, including trivial edges when the leaf was already a leaf of T:

    Delta_B(T) = sum_c P_c tensor R_c.
    Delta_B(e_J) = e_J tensor e_J.

Extend multiplicatively to forests. The counit is 1 on every nodeless forest and 0 on every positive-degree forest.

A nullary ground g:()->J has:

    Delta_B(g) = 1 tensor g + g tensor e_J.

A unary rule f:J->K and ground g:()->J have:

    Delta_B(f(g)) = 1 tensor f(g)
                  + g tensor f(e_J)
                  + f(g) tensor e_K.

The open corolla differs:

    Delta_B(f(e_J)) = e_J tensor f(e_J)
                    + f(e_J) tensor e_K.

Coassociativity is the bijection between flags of arity-preserving root subtrees. Equivalently, a three-layer division can be assembled by first choosing the upper boundary or the lower boundary. Ordered forest positions are retained by both constructions. This is the ordered/planar version of the standard P-tree argument, rather than an attribution that the cited commutative theorem itself is noncommutative.

## 3. Connected quotient and exact typed coaction

Let I be the two-sided ideal generated by e_J-1. It is a biideal since

    Delta_B(e_J-1) = (e_J-1) tensor e_J + 1 tensor (e_J-1),
    epsilon(e_J-1)=0.

Hence q:B->H=B/I is a bialgebra map. H is connected in positive-vertex degree, so has the usual recursive antipode. Its basis can be taken to be ordered forests of positive typed trees. Distinct positive tree generators remain distinct; only separate trivial-edge factors disappear.

Define

    lambda = (q tensor id_B) Delta_B : B -> H tensor B.

Then

    (Delta_H tensor id_B) lambda
      = (q tensor q tensor id_B)(Delta_B tensor id_B)Delta_B
      = (id_H tensor lambda)lambda,
    (epsilon_H tensor id_B)lambda = id_B.

Thus B is a genuine counital left H-comodule. In particular:

    lambda(e_J)=1 tensor e_J,
    lambda(g)=1 tensor g + g tensor e_J,
    lambda(f(e_J))=1 tensor f(e_J)+f(e_J) tensor e_K.

Its whole-tree endpoint has a typed root vacancy on the right, not the empty forest. The ordinary connected coproduct on H instead uses (q tensor q)Delta_B, giving the endpoint T tensor 1. These are different lawful constructions.

## 4. Material selection and root-protected native cuts

Choose a subset M of rule instances. Let H_M be generated by positive trees all of whose vertices lie in M. Typed cuts preserve purity, so H_M is a sub-Hopf algebra. The map pi:H->H_M that retains wholly M-pure forests and kills every forest containing a non-M vertex is a Hopf projection: every coproduct term partitions the positive vertices between its two factors, so an impure source has at least one impure factor in every term.

Then

    rho_M=(pi tensor id_B)lambda

is a counital H_M-coaction. The typed leaf/edge residues remain on the right, while coefficient factors are positive forests.

For a positive tree with root outside M, the whole-tree term is killed. The remaining terms are exactly the empty cut and antichains of nonroot positive subtree roots whose ideal trees are all M-pure. Existing leaf edges are not additional detachable positive components. Their compulsory trivial coefficient factors disappear under q.

The key combinatorial check: there is no independent choice whether to 'cut' an inherited empty leaf. A cut is its root-containing arity-preserving subtree, so a retained rule forces its entire incidence edges; an inherited empty leaf is a fixed edge of that subtree. Conversely a chosen antichain of positive nonroot addresses determines one such root subtree. Therefore quotienting compulsory trivial coefficient edges introduces no duplicate cut witnesses. Different actual address cuts can still collect to the same tensor basis pair, and those genuine multiplicities must be kept.

Examples:

* g:()->J with g material, root protected by nonmaterial c:J->K: rho_M(c(g))=1 tensor c(g)+g tensor c(e_J).
* binary material r:J,J->J, material ground g, nonmaterial c: the coefficient r(g,g) in rho_M(c(r(g,g))) is nonprimitive. Its coproduct contains g tensor r(e_J,g), g tensor r(g,e_J), and gg tensor r(e_J,e_J), besides endpoints. The two single-g terms have different residual ports, and must not be identified unless the chosen logical observation justifies it.
* c(r(e_J,g)) has one inherited hole and one positive g. The inherited e_J contributes no optional factor/cut. Extraction of g leaves c(r(e_J,e_J)) with its two different inherited/new addresses available in a witness-level refinement.

Pure roots require either the lawful typed whole endpoint above or a separately specified root-protected comodule. Do not silently identify the typed endpoint with a native nonroot cut.

## 5. Explicit failure of a tempting half-erasure

A naive endomorphism of B deletes trivial edges from *detached factors* but keeps them as genuine coefficient generators when their own coproduct is evaluated. Write d for this half-erased 'coproduct'. For the trees above it gives

    d(g)=1 tensor g+g tensor e_J,
    d(f(g))=1 tensor f(g)+g tensor f(e_J)+f(g) tensor e_K,
    d(f(e_J))=1 tensor f(e_J)+f(e_J) tensor e_K,
    d(e_J)=e_J tensor e_J.

The difference of the two coassociativity sides at f(g) includes exactly

    g tensor (e_J-1) tensor f(e_J),

and is nonzero in the free typed carrier. The cure is to use the connected quotient in the **coefficient coalgebra**, as lambda does, or retain all compulsory edge residues throughout Delta_B. This is a two-positive-vertex obstruction, not an issue that appears only with large proofs.

## 6. Fixed arity is not an ordinary Hochschild cocycle

Let f have exactly one premise. Extend grafting to a linear operator F on H by setting F(a)=f(a) for single trees and F(ab)=0 on a two-tree forest (invalid arity). If F(1)=0, the claimed ordinary cocycle formula already fails on a ground g because Delta f(g) includes g tensor f(e_J), but the formula does not.

Even choosing F(1)=f(e_J) does not repair the global claim. For two distinct admissible grounds a,b, the asserted identity on ab would require 0 to contain

    a tensor f(b) + b tensor f(a) + ab tensor f(e_J).

Those are nonzero distinct basis terms. Typed constructor equations must therefore preserve the exact port interface; they are not ordinary unrestricted B_+ identities. This does not invalidate CK coassociativity or the typed coaction.

## 7. D4 consequence and exact scope

A typed universal residual can be defined directly from the positive support relation of rho_M:

    A \ C = {R : every T and material P in A with [P tensor R]rho_M(T)>0 has T in C}.

It is the right residual of the H_M-assertion action on B-assertions. The coaction proves associativity of this action against CK coefficient convolution. This is not an assertion that B is connected or that the right action is an internal binary multiplication on one powerset.

A different residual for filling designated holes in a fixed typed context is also legitimate, but has a different quantification domain. The proof of finite recognizability must state which relation it compiles. Keeping typed right residues avoids confusing a whole vacancy e_J with the empty forest. Neither finite recognizability nor a safe residual gives Cut elimination without an additional, independently stated logical property.

## Sources and attribution

Primary sources checked directly:

* Joachim Kock, Categorification of Hopf algebras of rooted trees, arXiv:1109.5785, §§2.3–2.8: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1109.5785 . Operadic cuts retain arities, include trivial frontier edges, and yield a bialgebra with grouplike zero-degree residues; the core map is a bialgebra morphism.
* Joachim Kock, Polynomial functors and combinatorial Dyson–Schwinger equations, arXiv:1512.03027v4, §§7.1–7.4 and introduction: https://arxiv.org/html/1512.03027v4 . It explicitly distinguishes connected quotient from the residue-sensitive bialgebra and warns that its grafting operators need not be Hochschild 1-cocycles.

The quotient-comodule bridge, material specialization, and the explicit two-vertex half-erasure calculation above are elementary deductions supplied in this audit, not claims of literature novelty. The sources commonly state a commutative forest algebra; the ordered forest adaptation needs the ordered flag bijection described above. No equation licenses forgetting native port alignment or claiming new Lean verification.

## Executable evidence

`check_carrier.py` independently implements the full residue-sensitive coproduct, connected quotient coproduct, quotient-left coaction, and material projection. It compares complete integer coefficient Counters, not Boolean supports. All 381 sampled forests passed each of four coassociativity identities (1,524 identities total), and the coaction counit passed. Samples include every generated unary/binary typed tree through four positive vertices and 169 ordered two-component forests. The script also checks the exact half-erasure associator on f(g). These are finite implementation checks supporting the paper argument, not a substitute for its general flag bijection and not native mechanization.
