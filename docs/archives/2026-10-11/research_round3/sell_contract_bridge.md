## Two carriers and selected material

Let S be the permission-completed SELL calculus with Cut, and T the original
calculus with Cut, at a fixed promotion preorder. Their exact rule-instance
registries give separate typed tree carriers B_S and B_T. Fix a shared selection
L of material constructors. Its reduced connected CK Hopf algebra H_L is common.
Examples may select a distinguished material axiom g and tensor introduction;
this permits nonprimitive coefficients such as tensor(g,g). The repair's
identity, Cut, modal rules, weakening and contraction are unselected.

The compiler F expands each new W_b/C_b into the checked old-rule macro, fixes
shared material constructors, and preserves every premise once and in order.
Each added macro vertex is unselected; each macro is positive and its extra
branches have no proof meta-holes. Complete object-language assumptions of a
nullary identity are not proof meta-holes.

The selected-cut bijection therefore gives

    rho_T F = (id_H_L tensor F) rho_S.

This equality retains the whole ordered detached forest and compiled typed
remainder, not only a support or cost annotation. Nonprimitive and simultaneous
cuts are included. Full CK coproduct equality fails because the macro creates
new logical cuts.

## Inverse-assembly lifting and exact residual comparison

Let A be any assertion on shared material coefficient forests and C any target
source-forest assertion. The inverse-coaction theorem says every T-assembly with
right factor F(R) fills exactly its typed leaf edges with L-pure providers, or
leaves them as typed edges. F preserves the complete ordered leaf interface of
R, and fixes every shared L-pure provider. Consequently every such target host
has the source lift R[P], with

    F(R[P]) = F(R)[P].

Conversely every source assembly maps to that target assembly. Thus

    F^(-1)(WP_A^T(C)) = WP_A^S(F^(-1)(C)).

This is exact for the actual material coaction, not merely for compiler-image
providers: the entire chosen material universe is shared. It does not cover
arbitrary target proofs as providers unless a further coverage theorem holds.
It also does not identify independently chosen source and target predicates.
The source predicate on the right is explicitly the target predicate's pullback.
To claim preservation of a natural semantic property in both calculi, prove
that pullback agrees with the independently specified source property.

The proof relies on the inverse typed cut geometry and the absence of fresh
meta-holes. Mere colinearity would not suffice for arbitrary inverse-assembly
relations. This is one substantive reason for proving the inverse theorem
rather than only recording a forward coaction equation.

This establishes an executable natural instance of the already known macro
