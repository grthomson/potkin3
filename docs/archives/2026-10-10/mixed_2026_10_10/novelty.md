## 1. Ordinary mixed proof equality already has strong antecedents

Andrew Graham Barber, *Linear Type Theories, Semantics and Action Calculi*, Edinburgh PhD thesis, 1997, https://era.ed.ac.uk/bitstream/handle/1842/392/ECS-LFCS-97-371.pdf . Chapters 4 and 7 develop operator-based linear type theories and their higher-order extensions. Chapter 8 provides translations between terms and proof-net-derived relations. Section 9.4, Theorem 9 and Lemma 9.4.14 (printed p.235; PDF p.239), establish normalization and characterize higher-order term equality by relational normal forms modulo vestigial equality. Corollary 9.4 (printed p.236) gives decidability for ILL(C).

This is an appropriate primary antecedent for a mixed shell equality observer. The project still needs an explicit specialization of its selected primitive marks, ports, binding and occurrence identities. A deterministic native readback is an additional algorithmic choice; do not attribute canonical native syntax to the source's normal form modulo vestigial equality. A generic proof-net invocation without a translation and reflection argument leaves a gap. Ordinary tensor commuting conversions and mixed equality decidability are not new contributions.

## 2. Existing modular proof-net theory is a substantive comparator

Gianluigi Bellin, *Subnets of proof-nets in multiplicative linear logic with MIX*, Mathematical Structures in Computer Science 7 (1997), 663–699, https://www.di.univr.it/documenti/AllegatiOA/allegatooa_28077.pdf . Definition 2(iii), printed p.676, defines a normal subnet through correctness of its complementary structure with a nonlogical axiom. Proposition 1 characterizes this by switching-path closure. Theorem 2, printed p.683, connects identical nets with permutations of inferences and exposes empires/kingdoms as subderivations.

Consequently, presenting proof regions as reusable modules with correct surrounding operations has major antecedents. The project's more specific claim concerns exact selected material trees, complete occurrence-sensitive residuals, all future implementations and coherent nested decompositions. Comparison must respect Bellin's classical MLL+Mix domain versus the present intuitionistic fragment. His results do not directly provide the refined CK equality or its finite-mode algorithm, and neither should the project claim to invent modular proof replacement.

## 4. Exact dependence on CK and Faà di Bruno

The retained coefficient object must contain every actual nonprimitive material subtree and admissible antichain cut. Replacing a formal nullary probe by a composite implementation is not a Hopf map: it must add internal cuts. The previously established outer–inner rule partitions a final cut into old uncovered roots and inner cuts in the unswallowed supplied trees. Composite outer factors can span supplied proofs. Coassociativity is what equates repeated extraction with the corresponding flags.

A client should therefore exhibit both an accepted mixed transformation and a rejected transformation whose difference is a composite CK coefficient with a meaningful residual operation. A primitive-leaf comparator should agree while the full observer disagrees; otherwise the example does not demonstrate the promised advantage. At least one accepted example must include a genuine nonempty two-stage extraction and an actual composite refill.

This makes full nonprimitive decomposition indispensable **to the specified invariant and guarantee**. An equivalently informative typed antichain incidence representation can express it too. It does not establish that Hopf notation is uniquely necessary, nor make the standard coaction or Faà di Bruno identity a novel abstract theorem. No new antipode consequence follows merely by implementing its recursion.

## 5. Practical certificate: justified and unjustified payoffs

Justified, once proved and independently checked: one finite certificate can authorize replacement of an open proof operation across all implementations admitted by the declared mode universe, with matching normalized material observations after later extraction and refill. This is stronger than testing a finite list of concrete implementations. A diagnostic coordinate can identify a material-region or residual-operation mismatch.

Necessary limits:

- Fresh selected axioms are formal probes, not automatically conservative base extensions. A failure becomes an actual same-base counterimplementation only after joint realizability of that complete mode is established.
- Fixed material selection must remain fixed. Promoting old nonmaterial labels changes the invariant.
- The complete correlated mode tuple is the certificate; independently linearizing coordinates can lose correlations.
- Ordinary logical normalization equivalence alone does not license replacement under the refined material observation. The sharp positive-arity collapse obstruction remains in force.
- A successful proof-operation certificate does not itself prove process deadlock freedom, runtime resource safety, or an integration into POTKIN/Racket/Lean. Those require an independently specified client theorem and actual integration evidence.
- A reference implementation and tests support a paper proof; they do not mechanically establish universal quantification.

## 6. Audit disposition

The externally verified literature supplies a credible ordinary mixed equality foundation and strong modularity antecedents. It does not, in the inspected sources, settle the exact finite CK-refinement theorem. Publication priority remains unresolved. The mixed extension should be presented as a carefully delimited concrete theorem with standard shell-equality machinery and previously developed CK/Faà di Bruno machinery explicitly credited.
