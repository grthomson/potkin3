# Independent adversarial audit of larger-carrier completion

Continuation, 10 October 2026. This note distinguishes a repairable presentation problem from a structural impossibility. It uses the recovered target register, the 10 October checkpoint, and `cut_cut_gate.md`. No claim of new abstract Hopf theory or publication priority is made.

## 1. The two different objectives

The earlier N_push obstruction concerns **permutations** of explicit Cuts. Its two source proofs are related by dependent Cut-Cut reassociation, but its normal forms sit on opposite sides of a logical lambda. This shows that particular rewrite system is insufficient. It does not prove that the material coaction cannot descend through a larger material-balanced permutation congruence.

A different objective would identify all ordinary linear beta-normal proof identities while retaining actual material coefficients. That stronger objective is impossible without losing positive-arity material factors. The following theorem makes the loss explicit even when a coefficient Hopf quotient is allowed.

## 2. Forced-loss theorem for full beta proof identity

Work in the existing occurrence-linear implicational grammar, with actual atomic primitive rules, unselected references/logical vertices, commutative detached forests, complete indexed endpoints, and actual material-origin residuals. Primitive applications preserve passive resources except for their indexed intrinsic atomic discharges. Consequently every pure material cone and its actual-origin holes have empty free context.

Let H be the actual material CK coefficient Hopf algebra on the realized cut-closed typed carrier. Let q:V→W be a linear proof observation which identifies every ordinary atomic beta instance and its well-typed material residuals. Assume that every relevant complete typed endpoint vacancy has nonzero q-image. Suppose a coefficient Hopf morphism π:H→K makes the coaction descend:

`rho_bar q = (π tensor q) rho`.

Then π annihilates every realizable positive material tree whose root primitive has positive arity. This includes its actual punctured material residuals.

### Proof

Let `T=r(T_1,...,T_n)` be such a pure tree, with n>0. Each T_i has empty free context and atomic conclusion; it may be a zero-vertex actual-origin vacancy. Fix j and introduce a fresh reference x of the same atomic type as T_j. Define

`D = (lambda x. r(T_1,...,x,...,T_n)) T_j`.

This is a valid linear proof with the same complete judgment as T, and its ordinary beta reduct is T. A pure primitive root cannot have an actually used intrinsic discharge: that would require an assumption-reference descendant. Thus the fresh x is a passive input, not an intrinsic discharge token. Other indexed resources remain untouched.

Every material cone already occurring within a T_i survives verbatim. Their mutual incomparability is unchanged. The only newly eligible positive material root is the outer occurrence r itself, whose complete factor is T. There is no exterior branch in this pointed host. Therefore every inherited antichain term cancels after applying q to its residual: the corresponding residuals are the same ordinary beta instance with actual typed vacancies. The exact remaining difference is

`(π tensor q)(rho(T)-rho(D)) = π(T) tensor q(square_J)`.

Because q(D)=q(T), the descent equation makes the left side zero. Over a field, and since q(square_J) is nonzero, π(T)=0. The proof works equally when some children are actual material-origin holes; no arbitrary hypothetical vacancy is introduced.

This is a coefficient-level obstruction, not a cut-count argument or a choice of rewrite strategy. It cannot be repaired by choosing another representative or signed correction to the right residual while keeping the same q and π(T) nonzero.

## 3. The sharp surviving coefficient quotient

Let J be the algebra ideal generated by all realized positive material trees whose root primitive has positive arity. J is a Hopf ideal on the stated cut-closed carrier. In every proper CK term of such a tree, the right remainder retains the same positive root primitive, with some children replaced by actual typed vacancies, and therefore belongs to J. The two endpoint terms belong to `J tensor H + H tensor J` as well. Multiplicativity extends the coideal property to the ideal, and connected graded/filtered Hopf structure gives antipode stability.

The quotient H/J is the commutative polynomial Hopf algebra on the realizable marked nullary primitive occurrences; each surviving generator is primitive. Any π in the preceding theorem factors through this quotient. This universal factorization is relative to the supplied realized typed carrier: unrealizable constructor trees are not silently added.

Conversely the nullary-leaf coaction descends through full linear beta/eta proof identity. Such reductions neither copy nor erase a material leaf, and all subsets of those incomparable leaf occurrences match. Removing the same leaves leaves beta/eta-equivalent complete residuals. Thus the bound is sharp for this coefficient-quotient problem.

The price is decisive for the project: intact positive-arity modules, their punctured intermediate factors, and their nonprimitive CK/Faa di Bruno decomposition flags are all lost. Full ordinary normalization cannot simultaneously be the proof identity and retain the intended free actual material coefficients with nonzero complete endpoints. A normalized **refinement**, restricted material-balanced normalization, protected decomposition, or explicit normalization defect is required instead.

This theorem does not claim that no enriched semantic theory of fully normalized proofs is possible. For example, remembering histories alongside a normal proof changes q; protecting cuts changes H; annihilating endpoints changes the observation; and choosing only the leaf quotient changes the retained information. Those are different mathematical requirements.

## 7. Purity-tagged island normal forms: substitution obstruction

The proposed island representative hides every maximal pure material island, fully beta/eta normalizes the opaque shell, and restores each island behind an identity seal. A purity-tagged variant Nhat keeps an entirely pure term literal and otherwise uses that sealed representative. This distinction can repair native whole-proof context congruence: a pure related filler must be identical, while an impure filler prevents a new pure cone from crossing it.

It does **not** give ordinary meta-substitution stability. Let u=x:p and v=(lambda y:p.y)x. Both are impure and have the same sealed-shell normal representative x. After substituting a closed material delta:p for free x, u becomes pure delta while v remains the impure identity-sealed delta. Their Nhat representatives differ. The companion example `(lambda x.r(x))y` versus `r(y)` has the same failure after y is filled materially.

This is not a problem fixed by regarding meta-substitution as an ordinary syntactic context. Explicit Cut insertion retains its logical shell; meta-substitution eliminates that shell. They are different operations in the very refinement being studied.

More generally, any relation stable under arbitrary typed substitution that identifies x with its variable-identity beta expansion identifies delta with its pure identity expansion. If it is also closed under selected material contexts, it then identifies `r(delta)` with `r((lambda x.x)delta)`, contradicting faithful material descent and a nonzero complete endpoint. Thus the desired stronger congruence must reject even that variable-identity beta equation. Full beta proof identity is impossible here for an additional compositional reason.

A claim that Nhat is the largest contextual refinement also needs its future-base quantifier: fresh admitted unary material constructors at the relevant atomic interfaces distinguish a pure term from an impure term with the same ordinary normal form. A fixed impoverished base may contain no such distinguishing context. Basis-equivalence quotients with nonzero classes must be distinguished from arbitrary signed linear kernels which may annihilate an operation.

## 8. A finite symbolic future-substitution direction (proof obligations)

A possible finite parametric refinement tests every subset S of the atomic free input occurrences: replace x in S by a distinct fresh selected nullary material parameter alpha_x at its atom; retain other inputs as references; record Nhat of the resulting term. Compound inputs are necessarily impure and need no pure case. There are 2^a cases for a atomic free occurrences. The parameters are selected formal material constructors, not unselected arbitrary future rules in a fixed old-base projection.

The proposed sufficiency proof splits an arbitrary simultaneous substitution into its actually pure and impure supplied proofs. Replacing a formal pure parameter by a pure proof preserves maximal-island boundaries; identical symbolic island expressions instantiate literally, including their additional internal CK/Faa di Bruno cuts. Impure supplied proofs cannot create new spanning pure cones. Their own maximal material islands appear exactly once, and ordinary beta/eta substitution congruence transports the normalized opaque shell. This must be proved with complete indexed contexts and capture-free intrinsic hypothetical discharge, not only closed constants.

For material-comodule closure, one needs coefficient separation, not an automatic intersection-of-kernels assertion. An existing pure coefficient P has empty free context and is unchanged by the future substitution. Fresh filler occurrence marks cannot manufacture P's exact old support. Extracting that same coefficient after every symbolic/general substitution therefore compares the corresponding substituted residuals. Rigid marked antichains identify their roots uniquely. This is the route to a genuine residual-stable equivalence; unmarked coefficient collisions need separate analysis.

Necessity would follow from future-base/substitution stability and the contextual maximality of Nhat applied to every selected-parameter extension. The resulting relation deliberately distinguishes x from its identity beta expansion. It could supply a maximal **refinement** compatible with future substitution; it cannot restore full ordinary beta proof identity. At this audit stage the construction is a candidate proof program, not a claimed completed theorem.

## 11. Correlated signatures versus linear kernels

There is an additional algebraic boundary when the new classifiers are linearized. At the level of individual proofs, Nhat equality is exactly equality of the cofree/island observation together with equality of the purity flag. It does not follow that `ker Nhat = ker Phi intersect ker purity` for the separately linearized maps. For distinct pure m,n, the signed vector

`m - Seal(m) - n + Seal(n)`

lies in both kernels on the right: each seal has the same Phi image as its pure payload, and the two purity differences cancel. Its Nhat image is nonzero. A correct correlated tagged observation uses `Phi(t) tensor e_purity(t)` on each basis term and then linear extension; the two purity classes occupy different copies of the coefficient observation space.

The analogous issue applies to finite mode tuples. Equality of complete tuples is a basis-level classifier; the kernel of its free-basis linearization need not equal the intersection of the kernels of separately linearized marginal modes. Cross-mode correlations can disappear in those marginals. The safe construction takes the vector space on full correlated signature classes and proves its actual material residual relation termwise by rigid coefficient separation. A theorem about the greatest arbitrary signed subcomodule is stronger than a theorem about the greatest basis equivalence and needs its own argument.

## 12. Exact recovered-carrier boundary

Read the actual August source text at `prior_sources/august_material_occurrence.txt`, around Theorem 5.18. Its D_lin palette explicitly includes both identity cells in addition to principal, logical-passenger and Cut-Cut cells. Therefore a full D_lin-compatible **pure-material** refinement retaining nonprimitive coefficients faces the identity-in-material-envelope obstruction directly; this is not merely a missing proof for the old N_push orientation.

However the August Theorem 5.18 is explicitly about the occurrence coaction `Upsilon_B^occ` with coefficient algebra `A_B^occ`, after omitting atomic reflexivity. It is not a theorem of full selected-pure-cone invariance. The new obstruction does not contradict it. An occurrence observer can record a positive-arity primitive r even while r has a nonmaterial descendant; the pure-material coaction cannot extract that whole cone. Such an observer is not the same as a coefficient Hopf quotient of the pure-material coaction considered in §2. The precise comparison failure is that normalization invariance of the occurrence observer cannot be lifted to preservation of the full nonprimitive pure-material factors with nonzero complete endpoints.

The finite-modes theorem additionally needs future material extensions to preserve the selected/unselected status of every existing constructor. Reclassifying an already present unselected r gives a counterexample: r(delta) and r(Id(delta)) have the same signature when r is unselected, but become distinct when r is newly selected. Fresh material constructors, formal probes and separating unary contexts are harmless; selection changes to old labels require an additional profile-indexed observer. This qualification was sent to the theorem author and added to its domain.
