## 2. Fixed native terms and the primitive comparison

Let a,b,c be three occurrence-distinct nullary old-material witnesses of the atom p. Let r and s be old-material binary rules p,p⇒p. All occurrences and input slots remain indexed. Set

T=r(s(a,b),c),
T'=r(a,s(b,c)),
W[X]=lambda f:(p multimap q). f X.

Both W[T] and W[T'] are closed proofs of (p multimap q) multimap q. Both are normal for the declared compound-beta/eta or material-push normalizers, so no unresolved logical reduction explains the distinction. Their full marked material occurrence inventory is identical. They have the same truth-valued support at every admitted future old-base extension, because each is already a native proof and the old rules remain admitted. This is not a claim that an independently accepted proof-equivalence relation identifies them.

Let E={a,b,c}. For each I⊆E let T_I and T'_I be the wrapped witnesses with exactly those leaf occurrences replaced by their complete material-origin holes. They have the same named typed hole interface and output judgment. Let V and V' be the free vector spaces on these eight states.

The fixed leaf-catalogue observer is

δ_C(T_I)=Σ_(J⊆E\I) x_J tensor T_(I∪J),
δ_C(T'_I)=Σ_(J⊆E\I) x_J tensor T'_(I∪J),

where x_a,x_b,x_c are primitive catalogue coordinates. These are actual leaf-cut sectors of the material CK coaction, with the explicitly stated coarse catalogue projection; no material interior is counted as an independent leaf token.

Define f_0(T_I)=T'_I. Then f_0 is a typed, invertible primitive-catalogue comodule comparison:

δ_C f_0=(id tensor f_0)δ_C.

Consequently every iterated primitive extraction diagram also commutes. This is an equality of raw right residuals *after applying f_0*, not the false assertion that T_I and T'_I are literally equal. The comparison also preserves the proof-irrelevant support-operation adapter at the matched complete interfaces.

## 3. Nonprimitive extension obstruction

Let P=s(a,b), retaining its exact occurrence support and complete judgment empty⊢p. Let

R=W[r(square_P,c)].

The actual full material CK coaction contains the exact proper coefficient

D_P(W[T])=R,

where D_P extracts the left coefficient consisting of that single material tree P. By contrast,

D_P(W[T'])=0.

The latter tree contains s(b,c), not s(a,b). No actual antichain cut produces the requested occurrence-owned P. The two factors cannot be identified by their common output atom.

Let M and M' be material-coaction-closed spaces containing all actual material residuals of the two wrapped witnesses. They may both be the entire ambient space of well-typed old-base proof operations, with the same registered complete seam namespace. In particular the target is allowed to contain R as a valid typed operation; the obstruction is its missing coefficient at T', not an artificially missing target type or namespace. Suppose a linear map F:M→M' extends f_0 and is colinear for the full old-material Hopf algebra with the coefficient map fixed to identity. Coefficient extraction from colinearity gives

F D_P(W[T])=D_P F(W[T]).

Therefore F(R)=0.

Now let e:M→O and e':M'→O be the aligned linearized proof-irrelevant support-operation adapters, with the complete source/target interfaces retained. The actual operation R is nonzero under this adapter: it accepts any supplied complete p-proof, combines it with c via r, and applies f in the logical wrapper. At the admitted base p is inhabited, and the resulting valid operation maps the unique supported input tuple to the supported output. In the free linearization of these typed support operations, its image is a nonzero basis operation.

If F were support-preserving, e'F=e would imply

0=e'F(R)=e(R)≠0,

a contradiction.

**Theorem.** The primitive-catalogue comodule isomorphism f_0, despite preserving every primitive extraction stage and the aligned support operations, has no extension to a full material-CK-colinear comparison that preserves the nonzero support-operation adapter.

The theorem remains valid if F is allowed signed linear combinations rather than deterministic native witnesses. Semantic nonzeroness rules out annihilating R. If one intentionally chooses an adapter that kills R, or changes the coefficient Hopf algebra to identify/erase P, that is a different and coarser verification contract; the conclusion is not asserted there.

## 4. Why the obstruction is genuinely nonprimitive

P is a positive binary material tree with actual internal cuts. In particular its CK decomposition includes

a b tensor s(square_a,square_b),

with the positive intermediate material constructor and complete input holes. Combining this with the outer coefficient gives the genuine three-stage tensor

a b tensor s(square_a,square_b) tensor R.

Actual CK coassociativity identifies extraction of P followed by its leaves with extraction of the leaves followed by the residual positive s operation. The primitive-only comparison f_0 retains the leaf stages but cannot supply the required intermediate s/a/b operation. Its target has the distinct intermediate s/b/c operation.

This is not an obstruction visible from the primitive multiplicities, root rule, complete final judgment, or every finite stage of the fixed primitive-leaf coaction. It uses the nonprimitive coefficient and its exact complete consumer. No new numerical weight or external performance metric has been introduced.

The finite client verifies the native terms, all eight primitive-state comparisons and their 27 primitive coaction terms, the nonzero source coefficient, absence of the target coefficient, and the explicit internal cut of P. The general impossibility of F follows from the displayed linear argument, not from finite enumeration.

## 5. Consequence for the principal carrier/normalization obligation

The result supplies a precise completion gate:

> A proposed normalization/permutation comparison validated only by a primitive inventory, or even by all diagrams of one primitive catalogue, cannot be promoted to the intended full material witness comparison without checking nonprimitive material coefficients and their nonzero complete residual operations.

The current material-normalization bridge meets the stronger obligation by transporting actual cuts termwise. The theorem explains a real failure mode that this stronger proof excludes. It does not prove that the particular terms T,T' belong to the desired sequent-equivalence class; they are a finite obstruction to an otherwise overbroad semantic lifting principle. A comparison with that independently specified sequent carrier still requires its own native translation and relation-level proof.

A fair non-Hopf baseline retaining the full typed intermediate contexts can detect the same missing s/a/b module and derive the contradiction directly. Thus strict Hopf indispensability is not claimed. The algebraic consequence is nevertheless substantive: an honest nonprimitive CK coefficient rules out an attempted support-preserving extension after every primitive-stage test passes. The antipode is not used. The nested compatibility law is genuine coassociativity, not an analogy between two unrelated derivative notions.

## 6. Weighted hierarchy review

The earlier weighted inspection law remains correct for q≥1, nonnegative additive extraction costs, and a cost model charging only previously extracted providers. Restricting a detected batch to a minimal newly enabled subbatch cannot increase cost; all proper subsets of that minimal batch are supported. Therefore the optimum is the minimum, over minimal gains C, of the sum of the cheapest max(0,|C|−q) provider costs in C. Finding or enumerating those minimal gains may be hard or exponential, so this statement supplies no new efficient optimizer. This hierarchy concerns the primitive catalogue and should not be conflated with the full nonprimitive extension obstruction above.
