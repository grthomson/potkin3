## 2. An exact standard antecedent: explicit substitutions

Delia Kesner, *A Theory of Explicit Substitutions with Safe and Full Composition*, LMCS5(3:1),2009, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0905.2539 (`turn63view1`, `turn64view0`), Fig1 printed p6, has propagation through application/abstraction and dependent substitution composition. In a linear instance where the substituted variable belongs only to the inner argument, composition plus removal of the vacuous outer substitution specializes to the project's Cut–Cut reassociation. The same paper's Lemma2.2 is full composition. Its §4 supplies more involved termination measures when substitutions compose.

This is directly usable as a **design and proof template**. It is not an off-the-shelf CK-preserving theorem: unrestricted variable-substitution evaluation removes a logical identity wrapper on a pure material argument, which may create a new selected material cone. A completion retaining that wrapper changes the rewrite relation. Its convergence, eta interactions and material-origin holes still require proofs. Similarly, ordinary beta equivalence is too coarse for material decomposition; citing confluence of ordinary typed lambda calculus cannot establish uniqueness of an intentionally partially evaluated form.

The parameter-guarded beta mechanism remains close to Paolini–Ronchi della Rocca's parametric lambda-calculus: variable inclusion, closure under admitted substitution and reduction. Redmond TLCA2015 §2 p290 records these conditions and its standard confluence theorem: https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol038-tlca2015/LIPIcs.TLCA.2015.288/LIPIcs.TLCA.2015.288.pdf . Again this does not automatically handle new Cut-commuting rules or material holes.

## 3. The Hopf construction that can be genuinely applied

Calaque–Ebrahimi-Fard–Manchon, *Two interacting Hopf algebras of trees: A Hopf-algebraic approach to composition and substitution of B-series*, Advances in Applied Mathematics47(2011)282–308, https://www.its.caltech.edu/~matilde/HopfAlgebras2TreesSubstitutionComposition.pdf (`turn63view0`, `turn64view1`, `turn64view2`). Section9, Theorem8, printed p294, relates extraction/contraction coaction Phi to the admissible-cut coproduct:

(id tensor Delta_CK) Phi = m_(1,3) (Phi tensor Phi) Delta_CK.

Proposition10, p295, makes characters act by Hopf automorphisms and infinitesimal characters by biderivations. These are established composition/substitution machinery, not prospective novel abstract identities.

The project's native marked, typed and binding-sensitive carriers are not simply the untyped trees of that source. To apply the theorem rather than use its analogy, one must specify the contraction object, typed quotient/root interface, admissible extracted subforests, zero-vertex identities, and multiplicities; then prove closure and the relevant cut/contraction bijection. A fully erased untyped-tree image can satisfy the standard identity while losing the exact operation and thus cannot by itself settle the logical theorem.

## 4. What would make the combinatorial Hopf structure substantive

The appropriate target is not an assertion that no other mathematical language can encode the result. Typed contexts or decomposition categories can encode the same incidence data. Instead, require a theorem whose retained observations and conclusion depend on the actual nonprimitive coproduct, not merely vertex counts or one-token polynomial coalgebras.

A suitable concrete completion would establish simultaneously:

1. Normalization descends through the declared expanded equation carrier and is strictly colinear on all actual material-origin residuals.
2. A typed outer–inner substitution operation survives normalization, with exact Faà di Bruno sectors: an outer region swallowing a filler excludes cuts inside that filler at the same antichain layer.
3. Mixed normalize/substitute/decompose diagrams commute when the exact transparency hypothesis holds; when it fails, the discrepancy is a sum of actual native admissible cuts with complete operations.
4. At least one useful logical consequence requires a nonprimitive intermediate coefficient—for example a certified composite replacement and its nested refill, with a proof that a specified strictly coarser primitive observer cannot recover the certificate.

Items1–3 strengthen the scientific role of CK because the theorem is a compatibility statement about two interacting decomposition/substitution operations. They do not manufacture a new abstract cointeraction theorem. Item4 supplies a logical readout; merely evaluating arbitrary numerical characters, displaying an antipode, or counting more trees would not supply it.

An antipode-based inversion claim must further state an actual inversion problem with independently meaningful input/output, connected filtration/local finiteness and a realization of signed coefficients. A formal antipode always exists in the standard connected setting, so implementing its recursion alone cannot meet that requirement.

## 6. Cofree normalized residual lift: exact internal duplication

For a left H-comodule M and any linear observer n:M→V (for example ordinary normal form), put Phi_n=(id_H tensor n)rho:M→H tensor V, with the cofree coaction Delta tensor id. Coassociativity makes Phi_n colinear. Its kernel is exactly the greatest H-subcomodule contained in ker n:

- counit gives n=(epsilon tensor id)Phi_n, hence ker Phi_n lies in ker n;
- colinearity gives subcomodule stability of the kernel over the ground field;
- if L is a subcomodule in ker n, then rho(L) lies in H tensor L, so Phi_n(L)=0.

This elementary universal-property argument supplies an explicit realization of the archived observer core. The latest September memo, `prior_sources/Hopf Algebras and Hypersequent Proof Trees/research_memo_parallel_material_ck_hypersequents_2026-09-10.md`, §41 (MATERIAL-SEMANTIC-CORE, lines16435–16485) already defines the greatest CK-residual contextual ideal contained in the base-extension observation kernel; §41.5 (lines16705–16734) explicitly extends the construction to arbitrary contextual observational kernels. The distinction between subcomodule and contextual ideal matters: an arbitrary linear observer n does not automatically yield an ideal under all proof contexts/substitution operations.

Therefore the formula Phi_n is a useful standard realization, not a new logical-normalization bridge or an independent proof-identity carrier. New content would require, for instance, proving its kernel equals a separately specified permutation congruence; proving it is compatible with the intended native typed substitution/Faà di Bruno operation; or establishing a new logical consequence not already supplied by the generic observer-core hierarchy. Defining equality to be equality of Phi_n and then proving Phi_n respects that equality is not such a result.

The archived §41 unrestricted unit-detour strictness witness was corrected on October9; that flaw does not invalidate the generic greatest-core construction or its duplication relevance.

## 10. Proposed finite material-mode completion

A further proposed observer tests each subset S of atomic free-input occurrences by replacing those Ref inputs with distinct fresh formal selected nullary probes, leaving the other inputs as Ref, and recording the root-purity bit together with the island normal form. There are 2^n formal modes for n atomic free inputs; compound inputs cannot be wholly primitive-material in this native atomic-base discipline. The empty mode alone cannot be complete for metasubstitution: x and Id(x) can share an island normal form but differ after a pure material argument is substituted under a material parent.

**Closest archived theorem.** September §7.4, “Profiled material-descent theorem”, already proves that a finite symbolic profile circuit can certify equality under every typed filling and outer context, assuming fixed material-reduced/nonmaterial skeletons and complete occurrence routing. Its completeness clause requires free separating profile inputs and a complete residual normalizer/separating model. September §24's internal/external interchange theorem (around lines4826–4836) repeats necessary-and-sufficient free-probe criteria with hereditary residual instances and a complete separator. Thus formal probes plus Faà di Bruno filling is established project method.

The possible new theorem is a **concrete finite complete material-status test**, with canonical island representatives supplying the residual separator and exact native passenger comparison. It must prove that every actual filler factors through the appropriate mode and that expanding a pure probe into an arbitrary material tree preserves the full nonprimitive coefficient/residual law. Impure fillers can contain material islands and may normalize internally; treating them as a single permanently featureless token is insufficient unless a substitution/reconstruction theorem preserves that hidden material data. A zero-vertex actual vacancy is also not a positive nullary probe or the Hopf unit; hereditary opening must account for it explicitly.

**Formal versus semantic completeness.** A fresh tagged nullary axiom with conclusion an existing atom p is a legitimate formal free-probe extension. It is not automatically conservative over an atomic base: it can turn p from unprovable into provable. Equality in all formal modes may therefore be stronger than indistinguishability by actual same-base fillers. To claim necessity for an actual base/future class, the relevant modes must be jointly realizable at their complete interfaces or an explicit permitted extension theorem must be supplied. This is precisely the old formal-probe/semantic-admission distinction, sharpened in October9's complete-judgment joint-witness criterion.

No exact 2^n atomic-material-mode canonical representation theorem was located in the inspected old records. The strongest justified novelty wording is that this would instantiate and make complete the previous conditional probe-and-filling framework in a specified native linear implicational domain. It would not create a new abstract contextual core, a new Faà di Bruno law, or unrestricted beta invariance. The intended relation deliberately separates x from Id(x) when pure substitution can create a new material assembly.

**Load-bearing Faà di Bruno obligation.** The expansion delta↦T for a nonprimitive material tree T is generally not a CK Hopf morphism: the nullary probe is primitive, whereas T has proper admissible cuts. A proof of finite-mode completeness cannot simply substitute T for delta in coefficient polynomials. It must use the actual outer–inner profile transformer, adding T's internal CK cuts and forbidding simultaneous inner choices whenever an outer cut swallows that filler. This is an explicit place where the nonprimitive combinatorial Hopf structure, or an equivalent full decomposition incidence structure, is essential to the stated theorem rather than decorative notation.

**Complexity phrasing.** If a is the number of atomic free-input occurrences and n is the explicit native syntax/interface size, the tuple has at most 2^a entries, each obtainable using the polynomial sparse shell procedure. Thus O(2^a poly(n)) construction/equality is a justified target with the declared alpha/interface canonicalization costs included; for fixed a it is polynomial in the explicit input. The compared expanded CK profile can have exponentially many cuts even when a=0. This is an exact representation/equality advantage over explicit expansion, not a lower bound against all other compressed representations, a new hardness theorem, or proof of exclusive Hopf computational advantage.

**Essential-use assessment.** The maximal compatible carrier theorem expressly demands descent of the original nonprimitive coaction. Its maximality proof separates a pure/impure pair using a composite coefficient with nonzero vacancy; the reconstruction proof uses the highest complete forest coefficient and its named residual; the implementation theorem requires internal nonprimitive cuts and excludes them when an outer cut swallows the filler. Removing these observations changes the theorem. That is defensible structural necessity. Conversely, the signature-equality algorithm itself can be implemented through material-island syntax and ordinary normalization, without expanding coproducts. An equally informed typed-context/antichain implementation can realize the same structure. The antipode is not necessary for these final results. Any claim that the results prove exclusive necessity of Hopf notation, antipode inversion, or numerical speedup over equally informed context representations would overstate the evidence.

**Priority status.** No exact complete finite material-mode carrier theorem was identified in the targeted primary/internal audit. This is not a certified publication-priority conclusion. It is now possible to state a narrow candidate theorem with explicit comparisons and falsifiable hypotheses rather than rely on a novel-looking combination of standard algebraic formulas.
