FIRST DELIVERABLE: A NATIVE FULL-PACKET CONTEXT THEOREM

Inspect existing installed-cut reindexing, native cuts/address antichains,
literal remainder/refill and multiplicity results. Reuse their carrier.
Establish a finite equivalence before introducing tensors or Finsupp:

  Cuts(C[P]) ≃
    (Cuts(P) × product of Cuts(S) for all sibling regions on the hole spine)
    ⊔ (sum over spine node k of product of Cuts(S) strictly above k).

The first case has no selected ancestor of the hole. The second selects the
unique selected spine ancestor k, which swallows the installed tail; there
are NO independent cuts in the swallowed payload or lower siblings. Prove
that the equivalence preserves the complete ordered pruning packet, literal
typed remainder, factor-to-hole matching and refill certificate. If macro
holes permute premise order, retain the explicit permutation.

After this equivalence, derive the native linearized identity

  delta ∘ G_C = L_C ∘ delta + A_C,
  L_(C[D]) = L_C ∘ L_D,
  A_(C[D]) = L_C ∘ A_D + A_C ∘ G_D.

The strongest next target absorbs the affine term using the counit. Set
e = epsilon tensor id and prove e L_C = G_C e and e A_C = 0. Define

  K_C = L_C + A_C e.

Then prove, on arbitrary tensor records including signed linear combinations,

  K_(C[D]) = K_C K_D,
  e K_C = G_C e,
  delta G_C = K_C delta.

The counit picks the empty-pruning contribution; adding the swallowed-payload
case once per input cut would overcount it. For several physical holes, apply
e to records in slots masked by a selected ancestor, keeping unmasked records.
Prove typed template substitution compatibility. Do not claim H-linearity, a
Hopf morphism, or a Beck distributive law without the additional structure.

Do not call A a Hochschild cocycle without specifying that structure. If linear-algebra dependencies remain
unavailable, prove exact finite indexed-sum/packet versions; report which
linearization is still blocked. Avoid rewriting the native carrier merely to
make a generic tensor theorem easy.

SECOND DELIVERABLE: ONE ACTUAL REVIVAL INTERFACE INSTANCE

Use Lang 2023 Definition 5 exactly. For each physical ghost-disjunction owner i,
its left/right slots share one colour x_i. Ghost p⇒ adds x_i=p at its assigned
left owner; ghost ⇒p adds x_j=p at its right owner; ghost ⇒ adds x_i=x_j. Every
owner exists even when no ghost leaf chooses it. Roles are actual ancestor
premise roles. Right leaves require a permitted empty-succedent checkpoint
strictly above their owner, including the empty ghost leaf endpoint.

Prove the fixed-assignment equality-network equivalence and one of the two
universal solver presentations, not merely a fixture evaluation:

  Rev_T(q) = sum over consistent, checkpoint-correct owner maps omega
               q^(number of unforced connected components),

or its exact local state/partition dynamic programme. A finite nonempty palette
containing all forced atoms suffices for existence. Rev_T(1) formally counts
raw ownership witnesses; this does NOT identify distinct forced atoms with one
colour. Certificate counts are not counts of distinct revived proofs.

For interfaces retain partition blocks, forced labels, the exponent for closed
unforced components, and checkpoint state. A seam shared by two regions is one
joint assignment, not two independent ones. A hidden free component contributes
q once. Existing ports remain live through constraint-free intermediate regions.

FULL MACRO COMPATIBILITY, AFTER THE PACKET RESULT

For a positive linear typed macro compiler f on coefficients and g on states,
define

  D = Delta_target f − (f⊗f) Delta_source,
  E = delta_target g − (f⊗g) delta_source.

Prove D,E are exactly the positive sums over target cuts containing a nonseam
selected root. Source cuts biject seam-aligned cuts. Include mixed seam/nonseam
frontiers. Require nonempty macros, one use of each premise, exact typing and
coherent origin/freshness transport. Then prove compiler composition and

  (Delta_target⊗id)E + (D⊗g)delta_source
    = (id⊗delta_target)E + (f⊗E)delta_source.

The coefficient defect D term must remain. A macro that erases a node or copies
a premise violates positivity of this correction; the notes contain exact
counterexamples. Copying is handled by a linear template in distinct physical
holes followed by the provider map/renaming, not by pretending f is a coalgebra
morphism. Native eligibility may weaken the all-positive classification; state
that explicitly rather than importing it unmodified.

Over the connected graded Hopf coefficients, also test the antipode transport
identity from macro_defect.md. With K = mu_target (S_target tensor id) D,

  S_target f = f S_source - K *_source (f S_source).

Keep this K distinct in names and types from the context-action K_C above.

primitive source generator expanded to b(c), the correction contributes
