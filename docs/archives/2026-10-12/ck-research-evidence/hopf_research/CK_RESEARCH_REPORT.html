1. What changed in the research direction

The previous inventory-factorisation result remains a valid negative result for its particular query. It does not settle the value of the connecting algebra. We now use observables that retain shared obligations, actual placement, and the effect of internal substitution. They separate examples that inventory or a whole-source census cannot separate.

The principal construction has four cooperating layers:

  1. Literal scoped syntax and all physical cuts. Proof/process trees keep occurrences, premise slots, inherited scope and typed holes.
  2. Open interface coefficients. Partitions record shared owner colours; pending-debt states record checkpoint obligations. Counting retains closed free components and certificate multiplicities.
  3. Proof-producing local macros. Revival constructs real cutfree LJ proof objects under WEM assumptions. Explicit structural adapters align reusable interfaces.
  4. CK and typed substitution laws. Convolution composes extraction layers; antipodes rebase cumulative cache data; context operations transport full extraction certificates; macro defects account for new internal cuts.

The Hopf framework is being used to derive and organise operations on these objects. We do not demand that an equivalent combinatorial implementation be impossible.

2. A genuine typed CK–Faà di Bruno framework

Lang’s grounded LQ proofs admit an exact local presentation. An edge state contains its sequent, the active ancestor-owner stack with colours and left/right roles, and the subset of right owners with pending descendant checkpoint obligations. Ghost leaves select actual owner positions. Ordinary nodes union debts and clear them at empty succedents. A ghost owner must discharge its own right debt before its conclusion can clear older owners’ debts.

For a closed occurrence-labelled proof, compatible colour-decorated grounding certificates are in bijection with locally typed trees of this signature. The debt annotation is uniquely determined; it does not introduce additional counting choices. This is an indexed polynomial grammar, not an analogy based merely on tree shape.

Let BP be its full operadic tree bialgebra. Its zero-node typed edges ei are group-like. The following construction yields both connected Hopf coefficients and a state space that preserves typed holes:

H = B_P / (e_i − 1),
M_i = free module on trees of output state i, including e_i,
ρ_i = (q ⊗ id) Δ_B : M_i → H ⊗ M_i.

ρ_i(e_i) = 1 ⊗ e_i.

Only the coefficient leg takes the quotient. An embedded typed leaf in a positive tree remains part of its syntax, and the remainder state ei does not become the coefficient unit. All positive-vertex extraction cuts remain present.

The exact cut/grafting bijection gives a genuine multitype Faà di Bruno identity. If Gi sums open trees with output state i, and gi,ν sums remainders with input-state profile ν, then

Δ_B(G_i) = Σ_ν (∏_j G_j^(ν_j)) ⊗ g_(i,ν).

Each physical input slot receives one matching tree, so the powers retain physical copying multiplicities. Closed accepted proofs form an acceptance class; they are not incorrectly treated as a cut-closed subcoalgebra. The full open-state language is essential.

This applies established polynomial-tree mathematics to the explicit certificate grammar. We do not claim that every such grammar contains a faithful unrestricted classical Faà di Bruno Hopf algebra. Completion and finite-fibre conditions are stated in polynomial_bridge.md. The compiler-aligned presentation is in debt_polynomial_bridge.md.

A further scoped-grammar theorem covers explicitly parameter-instantiated 2-systems of Ciabattoni–Genco. It preserves repeated top occurrences, declared parameter sharing and varying local contexts; it does not assume a scheduling normal form. Translating this presentation to the native POTKIN3 scope encoding remains a separate theorem.

3. Revival interfaces now compute and produce proofs

Exact certificate algebra

For a fixed routing assignment, identify each owner’s two slots with one variable xi. A ghost p⇒ forces its left owner to p; ⇒p forces its right owner; an empty ghost equates its selected left and right owners. Isolated owners still exist. A consistent assignment with k unforced connected components has exactly qk owner colourings.

Rev_T(q) = Σ_(consistent, checkpoint-correct routings ω) q^(k(ω)).

Open summaries retain boundary partitions, forced atom labels and the exponent for closed unforced components. Gluing joins partitions and rejects conflicting labels. A newly closed unforced component contributes one factor q. Shared external ports are matched jointly, not closed independently.

A symbolic partition-state dynamic programme computes the entire polynomial without enumerating every global routing or palette assignment. Formal RevT(1) counts raw ownership witnesses: distinct forced labels remain distinct in the symbolic constraints. Any nonempty palette containing the forced atoms suffices for certificate existence.

A useful additional interface connection

Pending-debt tables and inherited-checkpoint acceptance tables are related by Boolean zeta/Möbius inversion. This sends union-convolution of debts to pointwise multiplication. For r external right owners, the standard fast subset transform therefore combines complete interface tables in O(r·2r) coefficient-ring operations instead of enumerating 4r subset pairs. Coefficient costs remain separate, and inversion requires every formal Boolean state and subtraction. This is a concrete use of an established nearby construction, not a claim of a new transform algorithm or whole-proof complexity bound.

An exact inventory separator

Two actual 43-node LQ proof trees have the same multiset of complete local inference instances. Their modules occur under different owners. One has a grounding certificate and the other has none. This query therefore does not repeat the previously closed inventory-only observer.

Constructive revival and structural accounting

The marked compiler constructs an ordinary cutfree LJ derivation with one WEM assumption per owner. It transports formula occurrences, converts right tokens at their first permitted empty checkpoint, and replaces ghost disjunction owners by ordinary disjunction-left. Each source proof premise is used once.

|compile(T)| = |T| + L + 2R + W_added + C_added.

Here L and R count left- and right-assigned ghost initials, including the respective contributions of empty ghosts. Added weakening and contraction have explicit local charges. The formula is for the marked compiler; the more padded canonical compiler has its own costs.

The compiler commutes literally with restriction and refill at every source CK cut when its interface decorations and macro order are inherited. Its expanded target has additional internal cuts; those are accounted for by the defect theorem below.

A genuine eight-node WEM ghost proof compiles to an eleven-node ordinary proof with no added weakening or contraction. This supplies a positive zero-structural-debt benchmark. It is not a blanket transfer to every substructural base.

Reusable canonical proof boundaries

Equal compatibility kernels alone need not give equal live formula multiplicities. The constructive repair fixes a finite palette A and uses a canonical boundary:

original antecedent + W(A)
  + one ¬colour(i) for each active LEFT owner
  + one ¬¬colour(i) for each active RIGHT owner
  + one raw colour(i) exactly when that RIGHT owner has pending debt.

Explicit W/C adapters normalize to this interface at every node. Internal owner counts and token multiplicities can vary while the compiled boundary type stays fixed. A cached entry stores both its compact kernel and a proof generator for its realised boundary states. This makes contextual replacement proof-producing.

4. The Hopf algebra provides a working cache calculus

Let φ assign each positive tree a finite sum of certified replacement choices, with boundary-constraint coefficients. Extend it multiplicatively to forests. Let α value the exposed remainder. The full-cut action is

C_φ = U_φ α,
(U_φ α)(T) = Σ_c φ(P_c) α(R_c).

U_φ U_ψ = U_(φ*ψ),
U_φ^(-1) = U_(φ∘S),
C_ψ = U_(ψ*(φ∘S)) C_φ.

Coassociativity gives composition of extraction layers. The antipode cancels previously included cumulative cache contributions. Rebase calculations retain shared constraints through cancellation; a final scalar feasibility or cost projection is applied afterward. This is an exact algebraic update theorem, not a proved asymptotic speedup.

A source-checked cooperative repair

In the 21-node LQ fixture, both original modules force one owner to q. Each replacement forces it to a different atom p. A single replacement conflicts with the retained sibling; the pair succeeds. Every one of the source’s 963 cuts is present. With only these module providers cached, the final compatible plan polynomial is

z^21 + u^2 z^11.

The least residual cost is 11 source rule occurrences rather than 21. This is the explicitly defined residual-source-checking measure, not measured Lean runtime or the size of the canonical compiled proof. The coefficient counts physical provider-choice plans, not distinct final proof objects.

Adding the new cache to an old q-cache has zero first-order sensitivity at the origin, but a nonzero second Hasse coefficient. Every single-module improvement test misses the cooperative pair. The higher physical-hole calculation detects it.

Connected sensitivity

γ_s = (∂_s φ_s) * (φ_s∘S),
∂_s C_s = U_(γ_s) C_s.

The logarithmic derivative is infinitesimal, so the last expression sums over single-connected-subtree cuts. Its coefficients already contain the interaction corrections; it does not discard simultaneous dependencies. A two-node calculation in cache_connection.md shows the antipode removing a spurious nested partial-cache term. Multi-parameter corrections satisfy the corresponding flatness identity.

5. McBride contexts act on full CK extraction certificates

McBride’s derivative is a type of one-hole contexts. We use those contexts literally. For a fixed typed one-hole context C, divide cuts of C[P] into those selecting no ancestor of the hole and those selecting one such ancestor. The latter swallow the lower payload and carry no independent internal payload-cut choice.

This yields a full packet theorem, including the pruning forest, literal remainder and factor-to-hole matching:

δ G_C = L_C δ + A_C,
L_(C[D]) = L_C L_D,
A_(C[D]) = L_C A_D + A_C G_D.

The counit makes this a genuine action on complete extraction certificates. Put e=ε⊗id and KC=LC+ACe. It selects the empty payload-cut record once when adding the ancestor alternatives:

K_(C[D]) = K_C K_D,
δ G_C = K_C δ.

The construction extends to distinct physical holes and typed linear-context substitution. It retains full proof data; compression to a smaller observable requires its own interface theorem.

Exact quantitative derivative and operational separation

For the extraction spectrum Q, the corresponding one-hole transfer is

Q_(C[P]) = a_C(D(P)) + b_C Q_P,
ΔQ_outer = a_C(D_new) − a_C(D_old) + b_C ΔQ_payload.

D(P) is the actual dependency interface. The second term is the ordinary position-sensitive contribution; the first is necessary when closure adaptation changes that interface. Multiple physical holes give a multiaffine polynomial. Identifying providers after retaining their separate positions produces powers and exact Hasse/Faà di Bruno composition laws.

6. Macro incompatibility becomes a controlled correction

A positive linear compiler uses each physical premise once and introduces at least one target node per source node. Source cuts biject target cuts at source-macro seams. The other target cuts form a positive complement, including mixed seam/internal frontiers:

D_τ = Δ_target f − (f⊗f)Δ_source,
E_τ = δ_target g − (f⊗g)δ_source.

These differences are positive sums on basis objects, not unexplained errors. They compose exactly:

D_(στ) = D_σ f + (u⊗u)D_τ,
E_(στ) = E_σ g + (u⊗v)E_τ.

(Δ_target⊗id)E + (D⊗g)δ_source
  = (id⊗δ_target)E + (f⊗E)δ_source.

The coefficient defect term is essential in the mixed equation. The defects identify the added interfaces which D1/D5 must check. They replace an overly strong compatibility demand with an explicit comparison structure.

There is also an antipode-transport correction. With K=μ(Starget⊗id)D and source convolution,

S_target f = f S_source − K * (f S_source).

Every additional cut is proper, so the target antipodes needed inside the correction occur on smaller pruning factors. This gives a well-founded correction procedure; it does not establish a faster algorithm in general.

Over the full positive-node carrier and integer coefficients, a positive linear compiler is a Hopf morphism exactly when every admitted operation macro has one positive node. Expanded analytic derivations generally need the correction. Native material eligibility can change this criterion and must be checked separately.

Copying and erasure are not covered by the positivity theorem. A copying macro is first expressed with distinct physical holes, then supplied through a provider map with explicit fresh renamings. The linear-template theorem handles its internal interfaces; the occurrence/Hasse law handles copying. The admissibility of that structural wiring is a D2/D3 obligation, especially over weaker bases.

7. Relative Cut elimination is now a concrete paper theorem

The inverse construction starts from an atomic-identity cutfree LJ proof with designated WEM assumptions. It erases their formula ancestors occurrence by occurrence, converts the relevant disjunction-left rules to ghost owners, removes unary dummy rules, and extracts a fresh grounding certificate. Contraction and shared-context branching preserve the ancestry bookkeeping. An empty checkpoint survives dummy removal. Nonatomic identities must first be expanded.

The resulting ghost proof is cutfree and retains only subformulas of the unmarked endsequent. Auxiliary palette-only formulas disappear. Equal formula text is not enough to select what is erased: two identical WEM assumptions can have different designated occurrences.

Combining forward revival, a supplied ordinary LJ Cut-elimination operation, and inverse ghostification gives a constructive relative Cut-elimination procedure, including globally grounded sources whose owners span Cut premises. It returns a new grounding certificate; preservation of the old one is neither assumed nor required.

A concrete ten-node ghost example has a Cut premise that is not independently grounded. It revives to thirteen ordinary nodes with one Cut; an explicitly checked identity-Cut reduction gives eleven cutfree nodes; ghostification returns an eight-node grounded WEM proof. This tests the genuine spanning-owner case. The general base normalizer is a dependency, not an implemented or mechanized claim in this session.

These are specific next obligations rather than reasons to discard the framework.
