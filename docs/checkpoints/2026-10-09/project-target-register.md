### E1 Relative material and core sequence

The fundamental theorem of Hopf modules gives H≅B⊗K via b⊗k↦i(b)k. Under this identification Π=ε_B⊗id. Consequently

\[
0\longrightarrow J\longrightarrow H
\xrightarrow{\Pi}K\longrightarrow0
\]

is exact and split as vector spaces by K⊂H. It is also an exact sequence of B-modules when K is given the trivial action; that module sequence need not split. It is generally not a comodule/Hopf-module sequence: J need not be coaction-stable and Π need not be colinear. K's ambient coproduct need not land in K⊗K. Interpretation: separate base-generated algebraic material from the signed independent core. Neither J nor K is a carrier of positive checked proofs.

In our commutative CK setting there is additionally a genuine Hopf-exact sequence

\[
k\longrightarrow B\xrightarrow{i}H
\xrightarrow{q}Q=H/J\longrightarrow k.
\]

Here Hopf-exact has its standard extension meaning: i is injective, q is surjective, ker q=i(B^+)H, and H^{co Q}=i(B), where H^{co Q} is defined by (id⊗q)Δh=h⊗1. It does not mean ordinary vector-space exactness at B.

\[

\xrightarrow{\partial_F}\operatorname{coker}F_J
\to\operatorname{coker}F\to\operatorname{coker}F_K\to0.

\partial_F(k)=[\beta_F(k)]\in J'/F(J),
