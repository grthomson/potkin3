A mathematical theorem and its native mechanization have separate statuses. Neither Lean checking nor examples establish literature novelty.

**Maintenance contract:** read this ledger before choosing another research target. Update the existing entry when its scope/status improves. Replace this same document on future updates; version history is the change archive. No unattended maintenance is implied.

## Established algebraic and semantic spine

### L0 — Typed CK Hopf carrier

**Claim:** the specified free typed positive-open-tree carrier admits CK coproduct, connected grading and antipode; nested cut ideals give coassociativity. **Status: PAPER**, original 1 October proof. **Scope:** formal whole endpoint distinguished from physical root selection; typed vacancies are not positive generators. Native pair-basis extraction/flags below are related realizations, not automatic verification of the whole Hopf package. **Next obligation:** an exact carrier/API bridge if this Hopf packaging is used in a native theorem, not another abstract existence proof.

### T1 — Base projection, coaction and antipode decomposition

**Claim:** hereditary vertex-local purity gives a Hopf retraction π, the left coaction ρ=(π⊗id)Δ, compatible base towers, and the coinvariant projector Π=m(iS⊗id)ρ, with the Hopf-module decomposition H_P⊗H^(co H_P) ≅ H. **Status: PAPER**, original T1 proof. **This is pre-existing substantive antipode mathematics and must not disappear from project status.** Purity must survive rerooting/extraction; boundary re-provability is not purity. The decomposition is algebraic, not inverse normalization or automatic proof repair. **Dependency:** L0. **Next obligation:** instantiate its hypotheses in the intended proof-theoretic application before claiming native/application payoff.

### T2 — Protected marked migration adequacy

**Claim:** protected addressed CK cuts, checked retained-corolla lifts and jointly compatible exact fillers correspond bijectively to marked target derivations. **Status: PAPER.** Root protection, an ancestor-ideal retained region, physical origins and joint compatibility are essential. Different plans can yield the same unmarked target. **Dependency:** exact typed filling/L0. N2 below supplies one concrete treatment of nonlocal joint compatibility; it does not universally make independent filler products sound. **Next obligation:** combine source-valid quotation/lifting with the scoped discharge client in a stated migration problem.

### T3 — Typed substitution and cut correspondence

**Claim:** a cut of an installed proof uniquely splits into outer selections and inner selections at unmasked physical holes; outer selections swallow complete payload regions. Factors, origins, literal remainders and refill are preserved, with substitution rebracketing. **Status: PAPER; corresponding native results LEAN-REPORTED** (`CompositeCutExpansion.compositeCutEquiv`, later native packet/flag bridges). Positive-root and pointed-inner conventions must be respected. McBride's derivative is a type of one-hole contexts; this cut/substitution interpretation is additional structure, not a quotation of McBride. **Next obligation:** none for generic correspondence; use it as a dependency.

### T4 — Weighted protected migration frontiers

**Claim:** a bivariate nonnegative frontier polynomial counts addressed protected cuts by newly retainable vertices and newly quotable physical interfaces; endpoint positivity characterizes the corresponding marked migration under adequacy/joint-filling hypotheses. **Status: PAPER.** It counts frontiers, not all quotations or proof witnesses. Requires monotone retention/quotation predicates; semiring evaluation is not a ring map from the integral Hopf algebra. **Dependencies:** T2, T3. **Next obligation:** independently validate the intended source quotation predicates; do not equate Boolean availability with a chosen compatible proof.

### T5 — Obstruction to strict full-CK macro compilation

**Claim:** a positive multi-vertex expansion of a primitive corolla has a nonzero reduced-coproduct defect. With the stated positive-probe and completeness hypotheses, strict full-CK preservation forces one-vertex expansion and bijective premise routing. **Status: PAPER.** Probe-richness is essential; copying uninhabited slots can evade weaker tests. This concerns physical proof-premise topology, not every formula-level contraction. **Next obligation:** apply the defect to a substantive compiler; do not demand that the implication permutation preserve its coproduct.

### T6 — Structural atomic base-extension bridge

**Claim:** in the specified set-based structural atomic bases with Ainit, Acut and arbitrary-context propagation, hypothetical support iff atomic derivability; with T2/T4, a protected old-base migration exists iff the relevant frontier sum is nonzero. **Status: PAPER.** The set-context and quotation hypotheses are essential. It is not a theorem for arbitrary multiset substructural logics, BI or hypersequents. **Next obligation:** a genuine source-specific extension, not re-announcing the atomic bridge.

### L7 — Composition and interpretation of extraction defects

**Claim:** coproduct defects obey K_(στ)=K_σ τ+(σ⊗σ)K_τ; corresponding coaction/comparison defects obey the stated typed composition identities. **Status: PAPER.** `macro_defect.md` Theorems 1–4 additionally give the positive seam-aligned/nonaligned cut interpretation and coherence for positive linear compilers; its later sections treat antipode transport and strict Hopf compilation.
