# Typed CK proof migration: formal proof ledger

**Date:** 1 October 2026. **Status:** paper-level proofs in a specified free, locally checked, typed-tree model. No new Lean proof or universal POTKIN verification is claimed. This ledger corrects the scope and hypotheses of the earlier research overview. Each result below states its carrier, proof method, and remaining obligations.

The mathematical object is an occurrence-resolved Connes–Kreimer (CK) cut calculus on checked proof trees. It records both the detached forest and a *typed, punctured remainder*. Two independent evaluations of a cut have different meanings: whether its retained constructors remain available in a base, and whether its detached interfaces can be proved again in that base. This distinction makes the Hopf algebra useful for certified proof migration. Typed substitution of contexts supplies the McBride/Faà di Bruno direction; it does not generally give a strict CK coalgebra map.

“Proved” means the argument below is universal for the stated mathematical model. It does not assert adequacy of that model for every hypersequent calculus, BI, HCP, program, or process calculus. In particular, no strong-normalization theorem follows here.

## 1. Carrier and conventions

Let $\mathcal J$ be a collection of **full judgement interfaces**. A full interface includes the sequent or hypersequent boundary and any ordered premise positions, resource occurrences, component indices, sealed incidence, scope, and provenance needed for local rule checking. Let $\Sigma$ be a finite-arity dependent rule signature. A concrete rule instance is written

$$r:(J_1,\ldots,J_m)\longrightarrow J.$$

Its premise tuple is ordered. A checked complete tree is freely generated by these instances subject to their exact input and output interfaces. A checked open context may also have typed vacant leaves $\Box^J$. Checking is **hereditary**: replacing a complete subtree at an existing premise by the corresponding typed vacancy gives a checked open context; filling a vacancy with an exactly typed checked proof gives a checked tree. We either quotient fresh vacancy names by coherent alpha-renaming or transport their identities explicitly. There are no unrecorded proof-global admission conditions.

For a positive tree $t$, let $V(t)$ be its finite rooted set of genuine rule-vertex addresses. Its root is $\epsilon$ and $u\preceq v$ means $u$ prefixes $v$. A proper CK cut is a prefix-free $C\subseteq V(t)\setminus\{\epsilon\}$. Write $P_C(t)$ for the commutative forest of subtrees detached at $C$, retaining before collection the lexicographically ordered address-aligned tuple $(t|_c)_{c\in C}$. Write $R_C(t)$ for the punctured, typed remainder. The empty cut contributes $1\otimes t$; the algebraic whole endpoint $t\otimes1$ has **no root-cut witness**. A bare $\Box^J$ is not a CK generator. Forest product means independent formal juxtaposition, never Gentzen Cut, Mix, Communication, or filling.

Let $H_\Sigma=\mathbb Z[\mathcal T^+_\Sigma]$ be the free commutative algebra on isomorphism classes of positive typed open trees (with coherent vacancy identities), graded by genuine vertex count. Its connected-tree coproduct is

$$\Delta t=1\otimes t+t\otimes1+\sum_{\substack{C\ne\varnothing\\ C\text{ proper CK cut}}}P_C(t)\otimes R_C(t),$$

extended multiplicatively to forests. The occurrence-resolved cut span precedes this collected tensor expression: collection may merge distinct witnesses and lose the alignment needed to refill.

### L0. Coassociativity and the antipode

**Claim.** Under the stated typed cut-chain convention, $H_\Sigma$ is a connected graded Hopf algebra.

**Proof.** A nonempty rooted ancestor ideal $I\subseteq V(t)$ (if $v\in I$, all its ancestors are in $I$) determines the cut at the minimal vertices outside $I$. Its retained vertices are $I$; the complement partitions into detached cones. $I=V(t)$ represents the empty cut. Add $I=\varnothing$ as the *formal* whole endpoint, without constructing a root cut. Each term of either $(\Delta\otimes\mathrm{id})\Delta t$ or $(\mathrm{id}\otimes\Delta)\Delta t$ is indexed, before collection, by a chain

$$\varnothing\subseteq I\subseteq J\subseteq V(t)$$

of ancestor ideals. The three ordered tensor layers are the **typed forest/context factors** on vertex regions $V(t)\setminus J$, $J\setminus I$, and $I$, each retaining original vacant leaves and acquiring frontier vacancies at cuts. Cutting the detached forest first or the retained context second produces the same factors modulo coherent address and alpha transport; the original address of each vertex and the composite vacancy address are transported through the chain. These two decompositions are inverse termwise. The endpoint ideals give the unit terms, hence coassociativity and the counit $\varepsilon(1)=1$, $\varepsilon(F)=0$ for every positive forest. Degree zero contains only the empty forest, because bare vacancies are not generators. The standard finite connected-graded recursion therefore constructs the antipode. $\square$

The argument needs the free typed-open carrier. Complete derivations alone are not closed: the cut of a complete $r(a)$ produces $r(\Box^{J_a})$. If a particular implementation stores fewer open contexts, L0 describes its mathematical envelope and requires a closure/embedding check.

## 2. Base-local Hopf projection and coaction

Choose a subset $P\subseteq\Sigma$ of **exact, vertex-local** rule-instance labels. The interfaces and incidence of every retained instance remain literally unchanged. Membership is stable when a subtree is re-rooted or an unrelated premise is punctured. Let $H_P$ initially be the subalgebra on positive trees whose every vertex label lies in $P$; the proof below establishes that it is a sub-Hopf algebra. Define linearly on forest monomials

$$\pi_P(F)=\begin{cases}F,&\text{every positive vertex of }F\text{ has label in }P,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$

### T1. Hopf retraction, left coaction, and base towers

**Claim.** $\pi_P:H_\Sigma\to H_P$ is a unital graded Hopf retraction. Moreover

$$\rho_P=(\pi_P\otimes\mathrm{id})\Delta_\Sigma:H_\Sigma\longrightarrow H_P\otimes H_\Sigma$$

is a **left** $H_P$-coaction. If $P\subseteq Q\subseteq\Sigma$, then

$$\pi_P^\Sigma=\pi_P^Q\pi_Q^\Sigma,\qquad
\rho_P^\Sigma=(\pi_P^Q\otimes\mathrm{id})\rho_Q^\Sigma.$$

**Proof.** A forest is $P$-pure iff each of its factors is $P$-pure, so $\pi_P$ preserves multiplication and the empty-forest unit. Every individual cut, including the two endpoints, partitions *all positive vertex occurrences* of $t$ between its detached coordinate and its retained coordinate. If $t$ is pure, both coordinates are pure. If $t$ is impure, at least one coordinate is impure, so that tensor summand is killed by $\pi_P\otimes\pi_P$. Thus, term by term and then linearly,

$$\Delta_P\pi_P=(\pi_P\otimes\pi_P)\Delta_\Sigma.$$

The counits commute and $\pi_P$ is the identity on $H_P$. Both $\pi_PS_\Sigma$ and $S_P\pi_P$ are convolution inverses of $\pi_P$, so uniqueness proves antipode compatibility. Applying $\pi_P\otimes\pi_P\otimes\mathrm{id}$ to L0 proves $(\Delta_P\otimes\mathrm{id})\rho_P=(\mathrm{id}\otimes\rho_P)\rho_P$; the counit law follows likewise. Finally, for each forest basis element, purity for $P$ is purity for $Q$ followed by purity for $P$ within $Q$, proving both tower equations. $\square$

The forest action through the inclusion $i:H_P\hookrightarrow H_\Sigma$ obeys the Hopf-module compatibility law

$$\rho_P(i(h)m)=\sum h_{(1)}m_{(-1)}\otimes i(h_{(2)})m_{(0)}$$

because $\rho_P$ is multiplicative and $\rho_Pi=(\mathrm{id}\otimes i)\Delta_P$. The usual fundamental theorem of Hopf modules therefore applies. More explicitly, in Sweedler notation the coinvariant projector is $\Pi_P(m)=\sum i(S_P(m_{(-1)}))m_{(0)}$. Coassociativity and the antipode law give $\rho_P(\Pi_P(m))=1\otimes\Pi_P(m)$; writing one extra coproduct layer, the relevant simplification is $\sum S(a_{(2)})a_{(3)}\otimes S(a_{(1)})=1\otimes S(a)$. The identity $\sum a_{(1)}S(a_{(2)})=\varepsilon(a)1$ gives $m=\sum i(m_{(-1)})\Pi_P(m_{(0)})$. Conversely, for coinvariant $c$, $\sum a_{(1)}\otimes\Pi_P(i(a_{(2)})c)=a\otimes c$, so multiplication identifies $H_P\otimes H_\Sigma^{\mathrm{co}H_P}$ with $H_\Sigma$. This is an algebraic decomposition, not proof repair or inverse normalization.

**Orientation check.** If $a$ is pure and $r$ impure, then for $t=r(a)$,

$$\rho_P(t)=1\otimes t+a\otimes r(\Box),\qquad
(\mathrm{id}\otimes\pi_P)\Delta(t)=t\otimes1.$$

The implemented detached-material interpretation uses the **left** orientation. The right coaction exists but tests a different coordinate. Address-dependent “purity” can fail the coalgebra law: if $r(a)$ is declared pure at its original addresses but detached $a$ is declared impure after re-rooting, the term $a\otimes r(\Box)$ survives $\Delta\pi(t)$ and dies under $(\pi\otimes\pi)\Delta(t)$. Semantic re-provability of a boundary is not $\pi_P$; it is a separate quotation predicate.

## 3. Protected proof migration

Fix a finite complete source proof $T$. Let $K\subseteq V(T)$ be a set of **concrete source addresses** containing $\epsilon$. A registry occurrence ID alone is insufficient: it can occur at several distinct addresses. Define

$$\operatorname{Adm}_K(T)=\{C\subseteq V(T)\setminus\{\epsilon\}:C\text{ prefix-free and }K\cap\bigcup_{c\in C}\downarrow c=\varnothing\},$$

where $\downarrow c=\{v\in V(T):c\preceq v\}$. Put $I_C=V(T)\setminus\bigcup_{c\in C}\downarrow c$. A **plan** comprises $C\in\operatorname{Adm}_K(T)$, a target-checked one-corolla-per-retained-vertex lift $L$ of $R_C(T)$ preserving each ordered slot and complete interface, and a tuple of complete target-checked fillers $Q_c$ at precisely the addressed vacancy interfaces. The tuple must be jointly compatible with the lift. A **marked target proof** $(U,I,\mu)$ instead chooses a rooted ancestor ideal $I\supseteq K$ of source addresses and marks in $U$ exactly one retained lifted corolla at each $v\in I$, at its corresponding ordered address; every remaining target vertex belongs to a complete subtree at an immediate missing source slot. No target vertex may be hidden between retained vertices or in an extra retained slot.

### T2. Migration adequacy

**Claim.** There is a bijection, modulo coherent renaming of fresh filler-local names,

$$\coprod_{C\in\operatorname{Adm}_K(T)}
\bigl\{(L,(Q_c)_{c\in C}):L\text{ a checked retained lift},\ Q_c\text{ checked fillers},\ \operatorname{Fillable}(L,(Q_c))\bigr\}
\;\cong\;\bigl\{\text{marked target proofs }(U,I,\mu)\bigr\}.$$

**Proof.** For each $C$, $I_C$ is an ancestor ideal containing the root and $K$. Conversely, if $I$ is any such ideal, its border

$$\partial_T I=\{c\in V(T)\setminus I:\operatorname{parent}(c)\in I\}$$

is prefix-free. Every $v\notin I$ has a unique first ancestor outside $I$, so the cones at $\partial_T I$ partition $V(T)\setminus I$. Hence $\partial_T I\in\operatorname{Adm}_K(T)$ and $I_{\partial_T I}=I$; also $\partial_T(I_C)=C$.

Map a plan to $U=L[(Q_c)_{c\in C}]$ by simultaneous checked filling, and mark the vertices $I_C$. Its positive vertices partition as

$$V(U)=I_C\;\sqcup\!\bigsqcup_{c\in C}cV(Q_c),$$

with the address prefixes interpreted through the preserved ordered slots. Conversely, read $I$ from the marks, put $C=\partial_T I$, extract $Q_c=U|_c$, and puncture $U$ at those $c$ to recover $L$. Local hereditary checking validates each extraction. The border identities and unique decomposition of a free typed term into a context and its ordered fillers show that both composites are identities. Root protection excludes the witness-free whole endpoint while the empty cut remains allowed. $\square$

**Necessary marking.** For $T=r(s(a))$ with protected root, cuts $\varnothing$, $\{(1)\}$, and $\{(1,1)\}$ can yield the same unmarked $U=T$ by identity quotations. The theorem is about *certified migrations*, not unmarked output proofs. An origin injection that skips a retained intermediate slot also fails the inverse decomposition.

If a proof-relevant base-extension semantics has an adequacy/reification map from support at each exact interface to a checked target filler, T2 turns support into a migration certificate. Boolean support alone supplies existence, not a canonical filler. If global freshness, budgets, channel scope, or bunched resources couple ports, use the joint compatibility set in T2; a product of separate Booleans is then invalid unless an additional independent-filling theorem is proved.

## 4. Typed McBride/Faà di Bruno cut substitution

Let $M$ be a checked **positive-root** open context with pairwise incomparable physical hole addresses $H$. Every hole $h$ has its own exact type and identity, even when several holes share one logical port label. Let $N_h$ be complete, jointly fillable checked trees and set $T=M[(N_h)_{h\in H}]$. The address partition is

$$V(T)=V(M)\;\sqcup\!\bigsqcup_{h\in H}hV(N_h).$$

The displayed $V(M)$ is the positive skeleton, not its vacant leaves. An *inner pointed cut* of $N_h$ is a prefix-free subset of $V(N_h)$ in which its local root $\epsilon$ is allowed. This option selects the **whole input at a nonroot position of $T$**; it is not a CK root cut of $T$ or of $N_h$.

### T3. Substitution cut correspondence

**Claim.** Proper CK cuts $C$ of $T$ correspond bijectively to an outer nonroot antichain $A\subseteq V(M)$ and, for each hole $h$ not below a vertex of $A$, a pointed inner antichain $C_h\subseteq V(N_h)$. Holes below $A$ are *masked* and have no inner choices. The correspondence respects the global cut addresses, ordered detached tuple, typed remainder, and simultaneous refilling. It rebrackets associatively for three substitution stages.

**Proof.** From $C$, take $A=C\cap V(M)$ and let $H_A=\{h\in H:\nexists a\in A\ (a\preceq h)\}$. For $h\in H_A$, set $C_h=\{u\in V(N_h):hu\in C\}$. If $h$ is masked, every vertex of $hV(N_h)$ lies below some $a\in A$ and thus cannot belong to $C$. Prefix-freeness of $C$ proves that $A$ and each $C_h$ are antichains. Conversely,

$$C=A\cup\bigcup_{h\in H_A}hC_h$$

is prefix-free: distinct hole cones are disjoint, and an outer vertex can be comparable with an inner vertex only by prefixing its hole, which $H_A$ excludes. The positive root of $M$ ensures that $h\epsilon$ is never the global root. The two constructions are inverse by the vertex partition.

For $a\in A$, the detached subtree is the **fully filled** $(M|_a)[N_h:h\text{ below }a]$; those fillers are masked. For $hu$ with $h\in H_A,u\in C_h$, the detached subtree is $N_h|_u$. Prefix every inner address by $h$, merge these roots with $A$, and lexically sort before making the commutative forest. On the retained side,

$$R_C(T)=R_A(M)\bigl[R_{C_h}(N_h)/h\bigr]_{h\in H_A},$$

where an inner root selection gives $R_{\{\epsilon\}}(N_h)=\Box^{J_h}$ as a **pointed input convention**. Structural induction on $M$ proves this equality with the fresh vacancy names transported coherently; filling each recorded detached tuple reconstructs $T$.

For the three-stage extension, now take each $N_h$ to be a **positive-root open context** with its own physical holes $k$, and take complete jointly compatible $P_{hk}$. Form $M[N_h[P_{hk}]]$. A final selected address lies in exactly one of the $M$, $N_h$, or $P_{hk}$ regions. An outer choice masks its descendant regions; an $N_h$ choice masks its descendant $P_{hk}$. Either parenthesization gives the same nonmasked labelled address set because address concatenation and simultaneous typed substitution are associative. The just-proved inverse from hierarchical choices to the final cut establishes the rebracketing bijection. $\square$

This is an **occurrence-level, single-cut Faà di Bruno substitution law**. It is not a claim that macro substitution preserves $\Delta$, nor an already-proved coaction law for arbitrary B-eS, nor an iterated-cut/decomposition-space theorem. Copying yields separate physical $h$; a shared DAG must first specify how use occurrences unfold or are summarized. Without masking, the false choice $A=\{(1)\}$ together with a cut below $(1)$ would select comparable roots.

## 5. CK frontier polynomial and certificate-producing base change

For each positive vertex $v$ of a fixed complete source tree $T$, write $J_v$ for the exact output interface of the source subtree at $v$. Let $p_v^B,p_v^C\in\{0,1\}$ assert that its one-vertex rule instance can be retained as a checked target lift over bases $B\subseteq C$. Let $q_v^B,q_v^C\in\{0,1\}$ assert that a **complete checked proof** of $J_v$ can be quoted over that base. Assume both predicates are monotone: $p_v^B\le p_v^C$, $q_v^B\le q_v^C$. These are different predicates: an old concrete subtree may disappear while its boundary remains re-provable. Assume independent, exact-typed filling as in T2. Define in $\mathbb N[x,y]$

$$p_v(x)=p_v^B+x(p_v^C-p_v^B),\qquad q_v(y)=q_v^B+y(q_v^C-q_v^B),$$

and let $\delta_v=1$ iff $\downarrow v\cap K=\varnothing$. For each nonroot subtree, recursively put

$$F_v(x,y)=\delta_vq_v(y)+p_v(x)\prod_{w\text{ immediate child of }v}F_w(x,y).$$

The empty product is $1$. Since $\epsilon\in K$, define the global protected-root polynomial by

$$D_T^{B,C;K}(x,y)=p_\epsilon(x)\prod_{w\text{ immediate child of }\epsilon}F_w(x,y).$$

In particular a nullary protected root has polynomial $p_\epsilon(x)$; its root is never selected as a cut.

### T4. Coefficients, CK incidence pairing, and semantic adequacy

**Claim.** The coefficient $[x^iy^j]D_T^{B,C;K}$ is the number of address-labelled admissible protected CK frontiers $A$ satisfying the following three conditions: every retained corolla is $C$-liftable; every selected exact interface is $C$-quotable; exactly $i$ retained corollas are $C$-liftable but not $B$-liftable, and exactly $j$ selected interfaces are $C$-quotable but not $B$-quotable. Consequently $D(0,0)>0$ iff there is a marked $B$ migration, and $D(1,1)>0$ iff there is a marked $C$ migration, provided T2's checked-filling hypotheses hold. When only quotations change and retained constructors are already $B$-valid, $D(0,u)$ counts $B$-to-$C$ quotation dependence; its least nonzero exponent is the minimum number of newly available **physical cut ports** in any migration plan.

**Proof.** More generally let $S$ be any commutative semiring and assign weights $p_v,q_v\in S$. For a legal frontier $A$, its retained ideal is $I_A=V(T)\setminus\bigcup_{a\in A}\downarrow a$. Give it weight

$$W(A)=\left(\prod_{v\in I_A}p_v\right)\left(\prod_{a\in A}q_a\right).$$

At a nonroot subtree $T_v$, every legal prefix-free selection belongs to exactly one of two disjoint cases. If it selects $v$, then it selects no descendant, is legal precisely when $\delta_v=1$, and has weight $q_v$. Otherwise it retains $v$, contributing $p_v$, and selects an independent legal frontier in each ordered child subtree. By induction on $|V(T_v)|$, the sum of weights in the second case is $p_v\prod_wF_w$, proving the recurrence. At the protected root only the second case occurs. This proves the semiring identity without any finite-fixture argument. With the displayed $\mathbb N[x,y]$ weights, each usable retained vertex contributes exactly one of $1,x$ and each usable selected port exactly one of $1,y$; unusable choices contribute zero. Multiplying and collecting proves the coefficient statement.

For the CK formulation, first retain the **uncollected occurrence CK span** $\Delta_T^{\mathrm{occ}}$: its records are the two formal endpoints and the proper cut witnesses $(A,(a,T|_a)_{a\in A},R_A(T))$, with original source addresses. Evaluate a detached factor at $a$ by its root's exact quotation weight $q_a$, making its value zero if its cone contains a protected address; evaluate a retained remainder by the product of $p_v$ at its original retained addresses. Give the empty forest value $1$. The formal whole endpoint evaluates to zero because the source root is protected; the empty cut gives $\prod_{v\in V(T)}p_v$; every other legal cut gives exactly $W(A)$. Therefore

$$D_T^{B,C;K}(x,y)=\bigl\langle q_y\otimes p_x,\Delta_T^{\mathrm{occ}}\bigr\rangle.$$

This is a **positive CK occurrence-incidence pairing**, proved term by term; the grafted root-choice decomposition is its typed cocycle-style recurrence. If original addresses, $p_v,q_v$, and protected marks are made *intrinsic vertex decorations transported by every cut* and disjoint origin identities are handled under forest product, the pairing descends to $(q_y\otimes p_x)\Delta\widehat T$ in that suitably decorated CK carrier. It need **not** descend to the ordinary undecorated collected tensor: two isomorphic detached subtrees at different source addresses can have different weights. No new proof-tree or cut carrier is needed to perform the occurrence pairing. A many-premise grafting operation is not automatically an ordinary unary Hochschild cocycle on the commutative forest algebra.

Finally, T2 maps each nonzero legal frontier plus selected checked lift and quote witnesses to a marked checked migration, and extracts a legal frontier from every such migration. The positivity of coefficients over $\mathbb N[x,y]$ makes the two existence claims exact. It counts frontiers, **not** distinct quotations, marked lifts, or unmarked target proofs. $\square$

**Algebraic caution.** The positive incidence calculation works over $\mathbb N[x,y]$ or another semiring. Do not describe this semiring evaluation as a ring homomorphism from the integral Hopf algebra $H_\Sigma$: there is no unital ring map $\mathbb Z\to\mathbb N[x,y]$ carrying negative coefficients. The antipode and signed convolution remain in the integral layer. If the retained predicates were merely $C$-valid and kept fixed, the constant coefficient of a one-variable quotation polynomial would *not* establish a $B$ migration; the two-variable formulation records that missing dimension.

For $B\subseteq C\subseteq D$, assigning distinct variables to first-stage and second-stage newly quotable ports gives the same induction and evaluations at $(0,0),(1,0),(1,1)$ for the respective base stages **when retained rules are valid already in $B$**. If retained-rule availability also changes, assign it its own stage variables as in the displayed two-variable construction. This is a filtration of **migration plans**, not yet a Beck–Chevalley theorem.

**T3–T4 substitution corollary.** In a completed $T=M[N_h]$ as in T3, let $G_h$ be the same weighted frontier sum on $N_h$ but **pointed**, allowing its local root to be selected because $h$ is nonroot globally; protection at any descendant still forbids a selection enclosing it. For an outer antichain $A$ in $M$ with no protected descendant below an $a\in A$, let $I_A^M$ be the retained positive vertices of the outer skeleton and $H_A$ its unmasked holes. Then

$$D_T=\sum_{A}
\left(\prod_{v\in I_A^M}p_v\right)
\left(\prod_{a\in A}q_a(J_a)\right)
\left(\prod_{h\in H_A}G_h\right).$$

The empty $A$ is included; the outer root is never selected. **Proof:** apply the T3 bijection to each proper final cut and factor its T4 weight by the retained outer vertices, selected outer roots, and independent inner choices at unmasked holes. If $a\in A$, its fully filled subtree is quoted **once** at $J_a$; all $N_h$ below $a$ are masked and contribute no separate factor. Conversely the three products determine the weight of the corresponding final cut. The formal whole endpoint evaluates to zero by root protection. This is the proof-relevant Faà di Bruno substitution law for the CK frontier evaluation. It requires physical, independently fillable input holes; it is not an unqualified scalar law for shared DAG uses or globally coupled resources.

## 6. Curvature of positive rule elaboration

Let $c_r=r(\Box^{J_1},\ldots,\Box^{J_m})$ be a source one-vertex open corolla. A *positive uniform macro translation* $\tau$ sends a source rule to one connected checked target open context $M$, maps typed vacancies coherently, and commutes with well-typed simultaneous substitution. It acts on positive forests multiplicatively and unitaly, is defined on all cut-created open factors, and transports exact vertex identity under subtree extraction. Here $n=|V(M)|\ge1$. Define its full CK curvature

$$K_\tau=\Delta_{\mathrm{target}}\tau-(\tau\otimes\tau)\Delta_{\mathrm{source}}.$$

### T5a. Graded obstruction

**Claim.** $K_\tau(c_r)=\widetilde\Delta M$, the sum of all proper target CK cut terms. If $n>1$, its right-vertex-degree $k$ sector is nonzero over $\mathbb Z$ for every $1\le k<n$. The degree-one right sector contains precisely the cut selecting every positive immediate child of the root, with coefficient $1$; it survives over any nonzero coefficient ring. Therefore a strict full-CK coproduct morphism of this positive deterministic kind cannot elaborate a corolla to more than one positive vertex.

**Proof.** $c_r$ has no positive nonroot vertices, so $\Delta c_r=1\otimes c_r+c_r\otimes1$. Unitality gives

$$K_\tau(c_r)=\Delta M-1\otimes M-M\otimes1=\widetilde\Delta M.$$

Choose a linear extension of $M$'s vertex ancestry in which every parent precedes its descendants. For each $1\le k<n$, its first $k$ vertices form an ancestor ideal containing the root. Its nonempty frontier is a proper cut $C_k$ whose typed remainder has exactly $k$ positive vertices. Thus that right-degree sector contains at least one tensor basis term. Every cut contributes $+1$; collecting equal basis terms adds positive integers and cannot cancel over $\mathbb Z$. For $k=1$, the only ancestor ideal of size one is the root, hence the frontier consists exactly of its positive immediate children and appears once, with coefficient one. $\square$

The argument is about full CK; a profile-projected coaction may erase the obstructing cut. An arbitrary signed linear image of a generator is not a positive connected macro and can exhibit algebraic cancellation.

### T5b. Conditional slot rigidity

**Claim.** Suppose, in addition, that every source premise type admits a positive one-vertex nullary probe that $\tau$ maps to a positive one-vertex target probe (or require the theorem naturally in a free extension by such probes); complete source proofs map to complete target proofs and every rule has a jointly inhabiting complete premise tuple; and no extra zero-vertex closed target filler is available. Then $\tau$ preserves the **full** CK coproduct on all trees iff each source rule maps to one target rule vertex whose ordered target premise slots use each ordered source premise slot exactly once, possibly after a typed permutation. Exact occurrence identities must move coherently with that routing.

**Proof.** Necessity starts with T5a, giving one target vertex $s$ for each $r$. Let $f:\{1,\ldots,d\}\to\{1,\ldots,m\}$ record which source physical input supplies each of $s$'s target slots. Completeness and joint inhabitance exclude a dangling, permanently unfilled target slot. Insert a fresh one-vertex probe $a_i$ into source slot $i$ and leave the other source slots vacant. The resulting source open tree $x_i$ has exactly one proper CK term

$$\widetilde\Delta x_i=a_i\otimes c_r.$$

Write $d_i=|f^{-1}(i)|$. If $d_i=0$, $\tau(x_i)$ is a one-vertex target corolla, so its reduced coproduct is zero while the displayed source term translates to a nonzero basis tensor. If $d_i\ge2$, cutting all $d_i$ copied target probe children yields a nonzero term of left degree $d_i$ and right degree one; the translated source reduced term has left degree one. Positivity rules out cancellation. Thus every $d_i=1$, and completeness makes $f$ a bijection.

Conversely, a one-vertex typed slot bijection recursively induces an isomorphism of rooted positive-vertex address posets on every tree, transporting all exact occurrence labels and puncture identities. It maps each antichain, ordered detached tuple, and punctured remainder to its target counterpart; endpoints are preserved. Summing this termwise correspondence proves $\Delta\tau=(\tau\otimes\tau)\Delta$ on trees, and multiplicativity gives the forest case. $\square$

**Why probes matter.** If a source has only $r:A\to B$ and no positive constructor concluding $A$, then its sole positive open tree $r(\Box^A)$ is primitive. Sending it to $s(\Box^A,\Box^A)$ copies the uninhabited input yet remains a strict coproduct map on that small available open-tree algebra. A free-probe naturality condition detects the copy. T5b classifies **proof-premise topology**, not formula-level weakening/contraction or external hypersequent routing hidden inside a one-vertex rule. A separate boundary/incidence naturality theorem is required for logical conservativity.

## 7. Exact atomic base-extension bridge

This section uses **only** the set-based atomic-sequent B-eS of Barroso-Nascimento, Piotrovskaya, and Pimentel, [*A Sequent Calculus Perspective on Base-Extension Semantics*](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10216280/1/978-3-032-06085-3_1%20%283%29.pdf), Definitions 1–3 and 6. In that paper, atomic contexts are sets, a base may contain nullary atomic axioms, derivability propagates arbitrary left and right atomic contexts, Ainit gives $\Gamma,p\Rightarrow p,\Delta$, and Acut composes $\Gamma_1\Rightarrow\Delta_1,p$ with $p,\Gamma_2\Rightarrow\Delta_2$. Its Definition 3 calls arbitrary-context rule application “Mix”; this is **not** Avron's external hypersequent Mix or HCP H-Mix. The paper's Lemma 5 gives a related fresh-axiom argument.

For finite sets $\Gamma=\{p_1,\ldots,p_m\}$ and $\Delta$ of atoms, write $\Gamma\models_B\Delta$ for its hypothetical support: for every extension $C\supseteq B$ and every family of **right-side** atomic sets $\Theta_i$, if $C\vdash\Rightarrow\Theta_i,p_i$ for all $i$, then $C\vdash\Rightarrow\Delta,\bigcup_i\Theta_i$. Extensions retain Ainit and Acut when $B$ contains those rules.

### T6. Atomic support/derivation equivalence

**Claim.** If $B$ contains Ainit and Acut with the paper's arbitrary-context derivability, then

$$\Gamma\models_B\Delta\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad B\vdash\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta.$$

**Proof.** Suppose $d:B\vdash\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta$. Let $C\supseteq B$ and $e_i:C\vdash\Rightarrow\Theta_i,p_i$ be arbitrary. Regard $d$ as a $C$ derivation. Apply Acut with $e_1$ and $d$ at the left occurrence $p_1$, obtaining $C\vdash\Gamma\setminus\{p_1\}\Rightarrow\Delta,\Theta_1$. Repeat for all $p_i$. Contexts are *sets*, so the side-context unions give exactly $C\vdash\Rightarrow\Delta,\bigcup_i\Theta_i$, establishing support.

Conversely suppose hypothetical support. Add tagged fresh nullary rules $\Rightarrow p_i$ to form $D=B\cup\{\Rightarrow p_i:1\le i\le m\}$. This is an allowed base extension; if an identical rule was already in $B$, keep its old occurrences tagged as old. Choose every $\Theta_i=\varnothing$ in the support condition. The added axioms yield $D\vdash\Rightarrow p_i$, hence $D\vdash\Rightarrow\Delta$. Take a finite derivation of that conclusion and replace every application of a **newly tagged** axiom $\Rightarrow p_i$ by the old Ainit instance $\Gamma\Rightarrow p_i$. Add $\Gamma$ to the antecedent at every derivation node. The paper's arbitrary-context application of each old atomic rule validates each transformed step; multiple propagated copies of $\Gamma$ coalesce because contexts are sets. The transformed root is $B\vdash\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta$. $\square$

Atomic Cut is essential. In a base with Ainit and the nullary atomic axiom $p\Rightarrow q$, but without Acut or any composing rule, $B\vdash p\Rightarrow q$; after adding $\Rightarrow p$, no rule need derive $\Rightarrow q$. Thus hypothetical support can fail. T6 neither proves an analogous statement for multiset-based linear/BI or hypersequent bases nor states that all B-eS support decisions terminate. At an exact atomic port $J=(\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta)$ it permits $q_B(J)$ in T4 to be calibrated by atomic sequent derivability, if a concrete checked quotation mechanism realizes that derivation. Quotations for compound, bunched, channel, or hypersequent interfaces remain separate adequacy problems.

### T6 corollary. A concrete atomic CK migration theorem

Let $B\subseteq C$ be structural **atomic sequent** bases containing Ainit and Acut, with the paper's set-valued contexts. Take $T$ to be a finite checked $C$ derivation in the paper's *derived atomic sequent system*. Each constructor is a fully instantiated Ainit, Acut, or base-rule application carrying its chosen arbitrary-context witnesses (the premise and conclusion context sets of Definition 3, or equivalent instance evidence), exact ordered premise sequents, and exact conclusion sequent. Thus checker admission and puncturing are local. Let $K\ni\epsilon$ be concrete protected addresses. Give each vertex $v$ the old-base retention indicator $p_v^B=1$ exactly when this *fully instantiated concrete constructor* remains admitted by $B$. Give its interface $J_v=(\Gamma_v\Rightarrow\Delta_v)$ the quotation indicator $q_v^B=1$ exactly when $\Gamma_v\models_B\Delta_v$ in T6. These indicators may be undecidable without an effective finite base, but are mathematically defined.

**Claim.** The protected old-base frontier sum $D_T^{B,C;K}(0,0)$ is nonzero if and only if there exists a marked checked $B$ derivation preserving the specified source corollas in $K$ and replacing disjoint nonprotected source subtrees at their exact atomic-sequent interfaces. When nonzero, any contributing CK witness plus a chosen $B$ derivation for each selected interface fills to such a checked result.

**Proof.** By T6, $q_v^B=1$ iff a finite $B$ derivation of the exact sequent $J_v$ exists; this is an existence *and reification* statement because the reverse proof of T6 constructs an atomic derivation. Every retained concrete $B$ rule instance is locally checked against the same ordered premise and conclusion sequents. Consequently, independently choosing $B$ derivations at the selected ports and filling the retained $B$ instances yields a checked $B$ derivation, by induction upward through the retained tree. This verifies T2's joint filling hypothesis for this particular set-based atomic signature. T4's nonnegative frontier expansion has a nonzero constant coefficient precisely when some protected CK frontier has all retained $p_v^B=1$ and selected $q_v^B=1$. T2 sends it to the desired marked $B$ derivation. Conversely, T2 extracts the unique CK border of the retained-origin ideal from any such marked derivation; each retained rule is $B$-admitted and each extracted replacement is a $B$ derivation, so the corresponding frontier has nonzero weight. $\square$

This is a theorem about **protected, marked, subtree-replacement repairs** of atomic proof trees; it does not say that every $B$ derivation of the same conclusion preserves the scaffold, that the repair is unique, or that a multiset/BI/HCP/hypersequent B-eS has the same quotation equivalence. It separates the semantic question “is this port supported over $B$?” from the syntactic question “may this exact old corolla survive?”, with CK supplying the simultaneous occurrence-aligned replacement certificate.

## 8. Measured horizontal and vertical composition

The following identities keep a coherent architecture without postulating a double category. Let $\tau:H_A\to H_B$ and $\sigma:H_B\to H_C$ be linear unital maps on compatible typed CK carriers. They may have nonzero curvature. Define $K_\tau=\Delta_B\tau-(\tau\otimes\tau)\Delta_A$. For any exact local base profiles with projectors $\pi_A,\pi_B$, put $\rho_A=(\pi_A\otimes\mathrm{id})\Delta_A$, $\rho_B=(\pi_B\otimes\mathrm{id})\Delta_B$, $J_\tau=\pi_B\tau-\tau\pi_A$, and $E_\tau=\rho_B\tau-(\tau\otimes\tau)\rho_A$. For subtraction, embed the relative left coaction codomains $H_{P_A}\otimes H_A$ and $H_{P_B}\otimes H_B$ into their ambient tensor squares. Coherent typing maps are part of the hypotheses.

### L7. Curvature composition and coaction defect

**Claim.** The exact identities are

$$K_{\sigma\tau}=K_\sigma\tau+(\sigma\otimes\sigma)K_\tau,$$

$$E_\tau=(\pi_B\otimes\mathrm{id})K_\tau+(J_\tau\otimes\tau)\Delta_A,$$

$$E_{\sigma\tau}=E_\sigma\tau+(\sigma\otimes\sigma)E_\tau$$

when the same intermediate profile is used in both stages.

**Proof.** In $\Delta_C\sigma\tau-(\sigma\tau\otimes\sigma\tau)\Delta_A$, add and subtract $(\sigma\otimes\sigma)\Delta_B\tau$; the two differences are precisely the first right-hand side. For the second equation, substitute $\Delta_B\tau=(\tau\otimes\tau)\Delta_A+K_\tau$ into $\rho_B\tau$, then subtract $(\tau\pi_A\otimes\tau)\Delta_A$. For the third, add and subtract $(\sigma\otimes\sigma)\rho_B\tau$. Each equation is an equality of linear maps, not a numerical pattern seen in fixtures. $\square$

These equations identify a measured base-change square: even when an elaboration is admissible, the square may have a nonzero, interpretable CK cut defect. Exact squares are a special case. The first identity is the substitution chain rule for this curvature; T3 explains it on **individual cuts**. They do not establish middle-four interchange for an objective double category, a full Calaque–Ebrahimi-Fard–Manchon tree coaction, or a normalization theorem. Those require additional structure and proof.

## 9. Proof accounting and next obligations

The carrier/coassociativity proof L0 underlies T1. The local checked filling hypothesis underlies T2 and the operational existence part of T4. T3 uses ordered physical holes and pointed root selection only *inside* a filled positive-root context. T5 uses the **full** CK coproduct and positive connected macros. T6 uses the cited **set-based** structural atomic system, not arbitrary substructural logic. L7 is an exact formal identity once all maps are well typed.

The proofs were built by (i) cut ideals and vertex partitions, (ii) inverse border/filling constructions, (iii) structural induction, (iv) a typed address-partition and masking bijection, (v) a one-vertex probe obstruction, and (vi) atomic Cut/fresh-axiom elimination. Independent skeptical checks found and repaired the address-vs-ID, old-vs-new retained-rule, missing-slot, masked-filler, left-vs-right coaction, and probe-richness errors. No test count substitutes for any of these arguments.

**Next universal proof target.** T6 gives the structural atomic sequent instance. For the intended substructural or hypersequent application, choose one tightly delimited calculus with complete interfaces (for example an Avron-style fragment). Prove that its native checker/reification realizes the local typed signature and the joint filling hypothesis, and prove an exact base quotation theorem for its interface class. Then T2+T4 gives a certificate-producing analyzer: it returns a protected CK cut, ordered detached tuple, quoted replacements, checked punctured remainder, and refilled target proof, or a finite stated obstruction relative to a finite quotation stock. T3+T5 classify additional cuts created by a derived Communication or other positive macro. A shared proof DAG needs an explicit occurrence/saturation policy.

**Further open theorem.** To infer normalization or cut elimination, specify a reduction relation on checked derivations and prove a well-founded, substitution-stable decrease for *every* reduction, together with an adequacy theorem connecting the CK/cut-frontier invariant to that decrease. Neither the Hopf coaction nor B-eS support alone supplies this. Avron's external Communication, HCP Mix, BI bunching, Gentzen Cut, and CK forest product have different rule meanings and must remain separate in that proof.

**Novelty calibration.** L0, the semiring antichain induction, Hopf-module theory, and the paper's atomic B-eS techniques are established tools. The candidate research contribution is their **checked, occurrence-resolved combination**: base quotations at exact CK cut ports, protected origin-marked migration, a degree- and port-sensitive curvature of structural-rule elaboration, and a certificate-producing program/proof analysis algorithm. These are now precise mathematical targets, not yet a literature-cleared publication claim. POTKIN Racket reports provide bounded implementation evidence; POTKIN3 Lean mechanization of this ledger remains open.
