14 October 2026 — paper development, independently reviewed; not Lean-verified.

Outcome
The next D3 bridge has been worked out at paper level. An independently inductive rule-discharge presentation corresponds to eligible top-to-bottom assignments. Naming bottom occurrences represents these assignments without identifying different source discharge groupings. A constructor-preserving encoding commutes with typed filling. Concrete native encoding and admission remain to be mechanized.

A sharper consequence
For the implication-right/system-bottom permutation, every possible discharge grouping transports bijectively because all system-occurrence branch/ancestry relations are preserved. Thus source admissibility is equivalent even when grouping is existentially hidden. This strengthening is specific to the permutation; generic summary replacement does not imply it.

This is a source-interpretation bridge for the existing structural theorem, not a new CK coproduct or a new normalization permutation. The current source fragment is identity, internal weakening/contraction, implication-right, and Sys(com). Full LJ and the entire hypersequent embedding are not established here.

Registry quantifiers

One fixed finite registry cannot admit arbitrary formula instances. Lookup IDs and system owner IDs are different; only the former are allocated for new descriptors.

Independent source semantics and grouping correspondence

D3 SOURCE ADEQUACY: RULE DISCHARGE BEFORE IDENTIFIER ANNOTATION
14 October 2026. Paper proof; NOT Lean-verified.

Desired theorem
---------------
For a specified ordinary sequent rule family R, finite Sys(com) derivations with explicit grouping of discharged top occurrences correspond to locally exact, globally legal designated-bottom trees, up to injective naming of bottom occurrences. The correspondence preserves every sequent, premise order, top occurrence, system parameter and discharge group. With exact per-rule encoding and registry admission this correspondence transfers the existing native replacement theorem to the source calculus. It is not an equivalence between raw ungrouped proof drawings and uniquely annotated trees.

Literature anchor
-----------------
Ciabattoni–Genco, Hypersequents and Systems of Rules: Embeddings and Applications, https://arxiv.org/html/1805.04852v1 Definition 2.1, Remark 2.1, Example 3.2, §4.1.1. The system permits repeated tops within each bottom premise, fixed shared multiset parameters, and unrestricted nesting before normalization. The restriction excluding two tops of the same instance on one path belongs to Lemma 4.2's NORMAL FORM, not original correctness. Consequently neither nearest-bottom discharge nor one-top-per-branch belongs in source adequacy.

Independent source presentation
-------------------------------
Fix ordinary sequents Gamma => Pi with finite-multiset antecedent and at most one succedent. Specify R by its actual inference schemes, independently of native CKTree/summary evaluation. For the immediate existing fragment take identity A=>A, IW, IC, and implication right Gamma,A=>B / Gamma=>A->B. Formula equality and multiset conventions must match the native presentation; any claimed larger R requires its additional encoding lemmas.

A source derivation has a finite ordered tree, exact R rule instances, Sys(com) top instances and bottom instances. Tops have these schemes for fixed finite multisets Phi,Psi:
top_1: Phi,Gamma=>Pi / Psi,Gamma=>Pi
top_2: Psi,Gamma=>Pi / Phi,Gamma=>Pi.
A bottom has two premises S,S and conclusion S, with a chosen system parameter (Phi,Psi). This parameter remains part of the instance even when no top is discharged. The following inductive rule-discharge construction defines grouping, WITHOUT naming bottom owners or inspecting ancestor addresses:

Every constructed partial derivation carries a finite set U of its still-undischarged physical top occurrences, each retaining its role and parameter instance. Occurrences are distinguished, even for identical rules/formulas.
* An R axiom has U empty. At an R inference take the disjoint union of its premise U's; prefix occurrence paths by the premise number.
* A top_i inference has its sole premise's U plus its new root occurrence, labelled (i,Phi,Psi).
* At a bottom B_(Phi,Psi)(D1,D2), choose finite subsets V_i of U(D_i), containing ONLY occurrences of role i and EXACT parameter (Phi,Psi). Discharge V_i at this bottom; return the disjoint union of U(D_i) minus V_i. Store these choices as the source grouping. V_i may be empty. No top is discharged twice because discharged occurrences are removed. Tops not selected may pass through this or other systems and be discharged farther down the proof.
A closed source derivation has U empty. An open source derivation allows typed holes; these are leaves with no present top occurrence. Unresolved top occurrences may be carried at its root. This representation deliberately distinguishes current unresolved tops from potential future tops introduced by filling.

This construction records genuine rule-discharge choices in the sense of systems of rules; it does not define source correctness by native legality or finite-summary success. Its equivalent geometric condition is a theorem below. Ordinary axioms/inferences remain independently specified.

Theorem A (grouping/ancestor equivalence)
----------------------------------------
Fix any finite locally correct tree T and the parameter of every bottom. Valid complete source groupings are in bijection with maps f from physical top occurrences to physical bottom occurrences such that:
(a) f(t) has the same parameter as t;
(b) t lies strictly in the role-i premise subtree of f(t), where i is the role of t.
Neither injectivity nor surjectivity of f is required.
Proof. From the inductive construction send each selected occurrence to the bottom selecting it. Selected occurrences are in that premise's pending set, hence that premise subtree; the subset condition supplies role/parameter agreement. Removal makes discharge unique; root emptiness makes the assignment total. Conversely, given f, process the tree bottom-up. In each processed subtree the pending set consists EXACTLY of those top occurrences whose assigned bottom lies strictly outside this subtree. At a bottom b select precisely the occurrences with f(t)=b in the appropriate premise. The induction invariant proves each such top remains pending and no other top is selected. At an ordinary/top node the invariant is immediate. At the root no pending top remains. These maps are inverse, including the literal selected occurrence subsets, not merely derivability propositions. QED.

Theorem B (faithful naming)
--------------------------
Let nu be any injection from the physical bottom occurrences of T into a supply of owner IDs. Label bottom b with nu(b); label top t with nu(f(t)). The resulting designated-bottom tree satisfies unique ownership and the independent global discharge condition. Conversely, any such legal annotation determines f uniquely by inverting the unique declaration of the referenced owner, hence recovers its source grouping by Theorem A. Naming is the only freedom if all non-owner metadata is preserved. Thus grouped source derivations correspond to alpha-equivalence classes of legal named trees, with alpha-equivalence restricted to bijections between the DECLARED owner inventories (including every bottom that discharges zero tops) and all their top references. There is no assertion that this bijection fixes arbitrary external names or extends capture-free to arbitrary future fillings.
Proof. Directly combine Theorem A with injectivity of nu. Every bottom inventory is finite, so natural-number names suffice, or use canonical bottom preorder ranks. The inverse preserves grouping exactly. Canonical redecoration may change numeric names, hence the round trip is literal only on canonically named trees, otherwise it is up to this explicit closed-tree renaming. QED.

Why raw erasure is insufficient
------------------------------
Take Phi=Psi={p}, S=p=>p, so a top_1 may have premise and conclusion S. Let inner bottom b lie in the first premise of outer bottom a; put one top_1 t in b's first premise, and close every other leaf with identity S. Both bottoms use the same parameter. The top t can be discharged at b or at a; unused bottoms select empty sets. Both are valid source groupings of the same raw unlabelled tree. The designated owner annotations differ, although every displayed sequent and rule label agrees. This example uses legal degenerate equal parameter multisets and needs no extra ordinary rules. It proves no unique reconstruction from raw trees, and no nearest-owner convention can be imposed without narrowing the source calculus. A nondegenerate example can be added but is unnecessary for refuting injectivity.

The native bridge and its exact proof obligations
------------------------------------------------
Theorems A/B do not themselves prove that POTKIN3's ExactInstance matches R. Let enc map each independent source rule instance to its native node data, exact premise boundaries, conclusion and ordered children. Require:
1. local soundness: every encoded source instance satisfies native ExactInstance;
2. local reflection on the image: native metadata retained by enc decode to the same source inference and parameter choices;
3. structural encoding preserves physical vertices, ordered premises, holes and sequents;
4. registry admission: each encoded instance is admitted by the chosen frozen registry.
Then structural induction on the source derivation, combined with A/B and the already mechanized independent Legal relation, proves source soundness/completeness ON THE ENCODING IMAGE. Completeness for every native Legal tree requires additionally that every allowed native tag/instance be in this image. Existing ten-instance admission is not enough to discharge (4) for a schema family. A per-proof finite registry can give a theorem parameterized by admission of precisely the finitely many source/target instances, without claiming one finite registry contains every formula instance.

This bridge should be mechanized for the existing actual fragment first, with R explicitly enumerated; a parametric proof over a locally adequate R can avoid duplication, but it does not credit unproved full-LJ adequacy. Adding full propositional LJ is a subsequent finite set of local encoding obligations, not a theorem obtained merely by naming R 'LJ'.

Open replacement transfer (safe statement)
-----------------------------------------
For source fragments with matched physical holes, encode with FIXED owner IDs and with every reference/parameter retained. If the existing native FixedOwnerInterface holds and all encoded source, target, fillings and exterior instances pass their respective admission checks in a compatible registry, native replacement gives equivalence of independent global Legal conditions for the assembled trees. Decode via Theorem A to conclude existence of valid source discharge groupings compatible with the retained designated references on either assembly. This is an existence equivalence, not a bijection of all possible grouping choices. The source encoding of a closed assembled grouping may be used directly; do not attempt to infer arbitrary future references from an unannotated empty hole.

More precise open formalization for Lean: keep the partially specified source discharge groups (including declared bottom occurrences intended to discharge future fillers), and require fillings to carry the explicit designated grouping references; this is ordinary rule-discharge annotation. Prove source filling/encoding commutation before transferring arbitrary-filling replacement. The currently proved native theorem has this information already. Erasing all grouping data before filling discards the information needed for the assertion.

CK relevance and limits
-----------------------
These adequacy results do not newly prove CK structure. Their role is to justify interpreting the existing native cut/summary/certificate laws as theorems about actual source rule-system derivations. Applying them to the already verified replacement theorem and implication permutation makes the logical conclusion independent of a hand-picked instance inventory. They preserve actual proof geometry and physical occurrences, so existing cut correspondence can transport along the encoding; no quotient by proof equivalence or identification of different groupings is licensed.

No full cut elimination, hypersequent equivalence algorithm, antipode use, automatic source adequacy for all R, or runtime speed claim follows. Literature novelty is not claimed for the annotation equivalence itself. The research increment is removing a source-representation gate from the previously proved CK-compatible modular replacement theorem.

Additional corollary: raw admissibility for the unary permutation
--------------------------------------------------------------
This corollary is stronger than merely transferring ONE fixed-ID summary equality to grouped source derivations.

Let u be any specified ordinary unary rule instance S/T whose side conditions remain valid when the same instance is applied separately to each of two derivations of S; implication-right in the existing propositional fragment has this property. The rule u introduces no system bottom and no system top. For ANY parameter (Phi,Psi), compare the source fragments
C(P,Q) = u(B_(Phi,Psi),S(P,Q))
C'(P,Q) = B_(Phi,Psi),T(u(P),u(Q)).
Here P and Q have end-sequent S. Regard their physical occurrences as distinct even if they are identical displayed derivations. Put either fragment in any unchanged typed source exterior and fill all corresponding holes by any same donor trees. Both assembled trees have exactly the same physical system-bottom and system-top occurrences under the evident map: the distinguished bottom moves, the payload/exterior occurrences retain their provenance, and only ordinary u occurrences are duplicated.

Lemma: this occurrence map preserves and reflects, for EVERY top t and EVERY bottom b, the relation 't lies in premise i of b' and the pair of system parameters/role labels. Proof by cases. For the distinguished bottom, the tops in P lie in its first premise on both sides and those in Q in its second; tops outside these payloads lie in neither. For a bottom inside P or Q, no top outside its own subtree becomes a descendant; its internal ordered geometry is unchanged. For a bottom in the exterior, both replacements occupy the same single contextual hole, hence all transported occurrences remain in the same premise branch of that bottom. The copied u nodes introduce no system occurrence. These cases exhaust the bottoms. Parameter and top-role agreement is literal. QED.

Consequently Theorem A transports ANY complete discharge grouping in either direction. Its two transports are inverse, since they send the same physical top-to-bottom assignment through inverse occurrence maps. Thus the two assembled RAW trees admit a valid source grouping iff the other does; indeed their grouping sets are in bijection. This quantified grouping corollary is justified here because the transformation preserves the full system-occurrence ancestry relation, not because equal summaries generally imply such a bijection. It extends to partial groupings with corresponding unresolved occurrences by the same argument.

An arbitrary fixed-owner replacement theorem does NOT automatically have this stronger conclusion: it can forget interior top choices and certify just the chosen designated references. State the universal grouping corollary specifically for the unary permutation, and avoid claiming it for all summary-equivalent fragments.

Native lift: instantiate the ordinary unary scheme and both bottom schemes in the registry, encode the source/target and matched donors/exterior, and use per-instance admission. Quantifying over all formula and parameter instances requires the schema admission bridge, not the present fixed ten-instance registry. No eigenvariable or global side-condition copying is justified by this propositional argument. The result applies in particular to Gamma,A=>B / Gamma=>A->B, with arbitrary formulas and antecedent multiset Gamma, once the formula-constructor encoding is faithful.

Open encoding and contextual transfer

D3 OPEN SOURCE TRANSPORT: EXPLICIT CONDITIONAL BRIDGE
14 October 2026. Paper proof, not a native Lean result.

Purpose. The closed grouping/annotation equivalence is not alone an open-source
adequacy theorem. This note supplies the missing paper-level filling lemma and
states exactly which native encoding premises remain to be checked.

Source diagrams. A diagram is a finite locally typed ordered source rule tree
with typed holes. Each bottom declaration carries a stable token and its pack;
each top carries a designated token as well as its role and pack. Tokens may
refer outside the current fragment. A diagram need not be a correct derivation:
duplicate declarations, unresolved references, and wrong-branch references are
permitted as input diagrams. At complete assemblies, SourceCorrect means there
exists an independent inductive discharge grouping from source_bridge.txt whose
selected bottom for each top has its designated token, with distinct bottom declaration
tokens and no undischarged tops. It is NOT defined by native Legal.

Filling preserves every existing declaration and reference token, substitutes
matching-sequent diagrams at physical holes, and prefixes physical occurrence
addresses by the attachment addresses. It neither freshens names independently
nor infers new discharge choices. Bottom declarations in different pieces may
collide; such assemblies fail SourceCorrect. Local inference instances remain
unchanged because the hole and inserted conclusion sequents match.

Encoding premises. Let e encode formulas injectively, encode source sequents as
singleton native hypersequents with explicit occurrence/incidence data, and map
each source rule instance to a locally faithful native constructor with the same
ordered premise list. It preserves declaration and reference tokens. The native
Imp relation must characterize the encoded source implication, not be arbitrary.
Each constructor's occurrence decoration is fixed before recursive tree encoding;
it must not depend on a traversal-wide fresh-number counter. Registry admission
is proved separately for the actual finite set of those constructors.

LEMMA — ENCODING COMMUTES WITH TYPED FILLING.
For every source diagram P and corresponding source fillings F_h,
enc(P[F_h]) = fillAssignment(enc(P), enc(F)_h),
with the canonical boundary casts and physical-hole correspondence.
It also commutes with insertion into a typed puncture context.

PROOF. Induct on P. If P is a hole h, both sides are enc(F_h). At a rule vertex,
source filling preserves the exact local inference descriptor and substitutes
recursively in each ordered premise. Constructor preservation reduces equality
to the induction hypothesis at each premise. Their hole maps are the same ordered
dependent concatenation; corresponding occurrence addresses concatenate the same
prefixes. Tokens and incidence descriptors are unchanged. The puncture result is
the same induction along its distinguished path, with siblings unchanged. No
freshness or validity assumption is used. QED.

THEOREM — SOURCE CONTEXTUAL REPLACEMENT, CONDITIONAL ON LOCAL ENCODING ADEQUACY.
Suppose enc(P) and enc(P') satisfy the mechanized FixedOwnerInterface, and source
donors match its physical-hole map. Then every completion by these donors and a
source exterior has SourceCorrect on one side iff on the other, provided the
assembled diagrams are complete. If they remain open, the statement applies to
every further complete filling under the same reference correspondence.

PROOF. By the lemma both assemblies encode to precisely the assemblies used by
fixedOwner_replacement. Closed grouping/annotation adequacy identifies their
SourceCorrect predicates with native independent Legal, on the encoding image.
Apply the existing native iff and reflect it back. For remaining holes quantify
over another completion and repeat. Checked quotations additionally need all
actual instance admissions; no quotation for an unfilled hole is fabricated.
QED.

This is a genuine conditional paper theorem. It removes an unstated naturality
assumption but does NOT discharge the concrete native formula, incidence, local
reflection or registry gates. Those are the next Lean tasks. It is not full LJ
adequacy and not equivalence of arbitrary ungrouped raw derivations. The special
unary permutation has the stronger all-groupings bijection proved separately.

Schema-general admission and application

D3: SCHEMA-GENERAL ADMISSION AND UNARY DISCHARGE PERMUTATION
Status: paper argument and Lean obligations; no new Lean verification claimed.
Evidence inspected: latest 14 October open-discharge report, including completed
continuation and its explicit ten-instance-registry limitation. Native source is
not present locally, so native API details below must be checked in POTKIN3.

1. The theorem we should prove

FORMULA GATE. The generic mechanized result quantifies over an arbitrary relation
Imp : Formula -> Formula -> Formula -> Prop. Source adequacy cannot inherit that
arbitrariness. For an independently chosen source formula grammar and encoding e,
prove both directions
Imp(e A)(e B)(e C) iff C = (A -> B)
on the encoded fragment (or an equivalent native constructor characterization).
If native formulas do not contain implication directly, exhibit its encoding and
prove injectivity/distinctness and this characterization. A schematic Imp predicate
with no adequacy theorem only proves a conditional result for a decorated registry.

SEQUENT GATE. Encode each source sequent as the appropriate singleton native
hypersequent, retaining context multisets and their local occurrence information.
For weakening/contraction, preserve the actual native incidence witness identifying
the added/contracted formula occurrences. Equal displayed multisets alone do not
prove equality of an admitted rule instance. All schema descriptors below include
the native incidence field; the registry construction is conditional on producing
that witness from each independently specified source rule application.

Fix the represented propositional fragment: identity, internal weakening and
contraction, implication-right, and the two tops and bottom of Sys(com). Fix
arbitrary finite formula multisets Phi, Psi, Gamma, formulas A,B, and system
identifier u. Set S = Gamma,A => B and T = Gamma => A -> B. Let R:S->T be
implication-right and let B(u,Phi,Psi;X) have ordered premises X,X and conclusion
X. Define two native open shells

C = R(B(u,Phi,Psi;S)(Box_S,Box_S)),
C' = B(u,Phi,Psi;T)(R(Box_S),R(Box_S)).

SCHEMA-GENERAL OPEN PERMUTATION. For arbitrary typed native P,Q:S and arbitrary
typed puncture context E expecting T,

Legal(E[C[P,Q]]) iff Legal(E[C'[P,Q]]).

The owner UID u is unchanged. All top references in P,Q,E are unchanged.
There is no freshness premise: duplicate declarations, inaccessible references,
and malformed fillers must remain equivalent failures. Local exactness and
registry admission are separate from this arbitrary-input legality equivalence.

ADMITTED COROLLARY. For any immutable registry Rg that contains exact validated
instances for the two bottoms and implication-right, and for donors/context
admitted in that same Rg, both constructions are admitted and the displayed
equivalence holds for their realized native trees. Each shell rule is checked
against the independently specified source schema; membership in an arbitrary
registry alone is not a proof of logical validity.

FINITE COMMON REGISTRY EXISTENCE. Given a finite schema-valid source derivation,
its finite transformed derivation, and finite schema-valid donors/exterior, one
can construct a single finite immutable registry admitting all of them, without
changing any system UID or rule-discharge reference. If an existing registry is
to be retained, use a conflict-free conservative extension of its finite instance
table and an explicit checked-derivation lifting theorem.

These are the correct quantifiers. A single fixed finite registry cannot contain
all arbitrary formula instances. Do not silently substitute the ten-instance
fixture for either quantified statement.

Use NativeDischargeReplacement.fixedOwner_replacement, not another bespoke
global event induction. Its FixedOwnerInterface is exhibited as follows.

Both conclusions are T. The two physical holes have boundary S and are ordered
left then right. The source addresses 0.0 and 0.1 correspond respectively to

3. Independent schema validation and finite admission

A rule descriptor records the schema tag, concrete formula/multiset parameters,
ordered premise boundaries, conclusion boundary, and the exact incidence data
required by the native registry. Sys(com) descriptors additionally record system
UID, role where applicable, and shared parameters. Exact logical validity is the
independently defined schema relation. It is NOT defined to mean that a registry
lookup succeeds, and it is NOT defined by discharge-summary success.

Take the finite list of descriptor occurrences used by the derivations. Allocate
fresh RULE-INSTANCE identifiers for these descriptors (deduplication of literally
equal descriptors is optional and unnecessary for existence). Store each with its
schema-validation witness. For a fixed existing registry retain all its bindings
literally and allocate new keys outside its domain. Structural induction on each
derivation constructs its quotation/admission witness from the lookup witnesses
and the admitted ordered children. Typed holes use the existing open-interface
mechanism; only complete derivations may use a constructor requiring completeness.

Rule-instance keys are lookup names. System UIDs express discharge identity.
Allocating fresh rule-instance keys never freshens or changes a system UID.
The source and target bottom descriptors usually need distinct keys because their
common boundary changes from S to T; they nevertheless carry the SAME UID u.
The two target R occurrences can use the SAME exact rule-instance key because
rule-instance keys need not be unique occurrence IDs. Physical occurrences remain
distinct through native addresses.

If the native instance-key type does not supply arbitrarily many fresh keys, the
extension theorem must assume the needed capacity or use the project's actual
schema-backed presentation mechanism. Do not claim an unverified allocation API.

4. Registry extension theorem and its exact limits

Define Rg <= Rg' to mean every old admitted exact instance lookup is preserved
with identical conclusion, ordered premises, payload, and incidence; the logical
signature/formula interpretation is unchanged. Under that relation:

(a) every Rg-admitted native quotation can be lifted to Rg';
(b) realization of the lifted quotation equals the old realization;
(c) all independent Legal facts and direct summaries are unchanged.

Proof of (a,b): induction on the quotation, replacing each membership witness by
the extension witness and keeping constructors/children. Proof of (c): substitution
along (b), since those relations inspect the actual tree, not registry membership.

This is conservative ADMISSION, not a theorem that adding rules is a conservative
logical extension. New derivations may be admitted in Rg'. No base-change/Hopf-map
claim follows. An inclusion that changes an old rule's descriptor is not adequate.
If realization embeds presentation-specific labels, prove exact forgetful equality
or the already available representation transport; do not assume definitional
equality across snapshots.

5. Repeated UIDs and contextual normalization

UID42 in the fixture is harmless for its one transformed owner. It is not a
universal owner name to reuse in a larger derivation. For multiple disjoint
systems, choose u1,...,un pairwise distinct, or retain the already unique names
from a legal input derivation. A local transformation retains its selected u.
The generic failure-reflecting theorem needs no added freshness assumption; if
the whole source is legal, its owner uniqueness already implies the necessary
noncollision with other bottoms. Source and target each contain exactly one copy
of this bottom, not both alternatives simultaneously.

Sequentially transform any finite chosen sequence of eligible unary/bottom redexes.
At every stage use the exterior-context theorem. Induction gives endpoint legality
equivalence and preservation of the owner inventory. Compile a common registry
from all finitely many stage descriptors, or extend incrementally with the lifting
lemma. This is closure under finite transformation sequences, NOT termination or
confluence, and NOT copying bottom owners. D1 cut elimination remains open.

6. CK connection retained, not reproved or embellished

Once arbitrary source/schema instances are realized as the existing native trees,
the existing summary cut-evaluation and certificate-gluing theorems apply. Their
hypotheses (exact local checks, owner uniqueness and completeness where stated)
are discharged from the source adequacy bridge. This is the main benefit of D3:
the CK theorem applies to a genuine rule fragment uniformly, not only a ten-rule
fixture. Cutting does not make detached factors independently closed. The native
scoped provider from the same remainder still supplies cross-piece discharge.

Do not state coproduct invariance of the permutation: its tree shapes differ.
Do not claim a Hopf morphism, antipode application, or full LJ source adequacy.
